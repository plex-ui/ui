A tip teaches a feature people might not find on their own: a title that names it and a sentence or two on how to use it. Show one tip at a time, close to what it explains, and stop showing it once the feature is used or the tip is closed. A tip is not for errors or for news about a task: use an [Alert](/components/alert) or a [Toast](/components/toast). It follows the TipKit kit, the macOS Mini Tip on a desktop and on a phone the shape TipKit's tip and most of Apple's own cards share, on the card of a grouped list or, for a popover tip, in the iOS 27 alert's glass. Apple's apps also draw cards of their own, with a large image, a centered layout or capsule buttons; build those from a [Card](/components/card) and [Buttons](/components/button).

### Tip.Popover

Takes `title`, `description`, `icon`, `actions` and `mobileActions` as Tip does, and the control it explains as its only child.

An inline tip is a note named by its title. A popover tip is a non-modal dialog named by its title and described by its description; it does not move the focus, so the control it explains keeps it, and it stays open while the focus moves. Tab moves from the control into the tip and from the tip on to what follows the control. Close is a button named Close tip.

## Styling

Tip reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it; at 767px and below the tip sets its padding, gap and Close tokens on itself, so override those on the tip:

- `--tip-radius`: the corners (default `20px`; `26px` at 767px and below)
- `--tip-padding-top`, `--tip-padding-inline`, `--tip-padding-bottom`: the padding (default `22px`, `16px`, `16px`; at 767px and below `14px`, the layout margin, `0px`, as the action rows reach the bottom edge, or `16px` under action buttons; the top value below as well without actions)
- `--tip-gap`: space between the message and the actions (default `22px`; `11px` at 767px and below, `16px` above action buttons)
- `--tip-title-font-size`, `--tip-title-line-height`: the title (default `17px`, `22px`, semibold)
- `--tip-description-font-size`, `--tip-description-line-height`, `--tip-description-gap`: the description and its space under the title (default `15px`, `20px`, `4px`; no space at 767px and below)
- `--tip-description-color`: the description and the symbol (default `var(--color-text-secondary)`)
- `--tip-action-gap`: space between actions (default `12px`)
- `--tip-icon-size`, `--tip-icon-frame-width`, `--tip-icon-frame-height`: the symbol and its frame (default `40px`, `44px`, `54px`; a `36px` tile at 767px and below)
- `--tip-close-size`, `--tip-close-offset`, `--tip-close-icon-size`: Close and its distance from the corner (default `30px`, `19px`, `20px`; a bare `22px` glyph 17px from the trailing edge at 767px and below)
- `--tip-arrow-width`, `--tip-arrow-height`: the arrow (default `47px`, `13px`)
- `--tip-background`: an inline tip (default `var(--alpha-05)`; the grouped cell, `--mobile-surface-grouped-secondary`, at 767px and below)
- `--tip-popover-width`, `--tip-popover-background`, `--tip-popover-shadow`: a popover tip (default `300px`, `var(--color-surface-elevated)`, `0 10px 30px rgb(0 0 0 / 20%)`; the alert's glass at 767px and below)
