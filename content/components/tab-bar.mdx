A tab bar moves between the top-level sections of an app on a phone, as the iOS 26 tab bar does: a symbol over a short label for each section, in a glass capsule that floats over the bottom of the content. Keep it to five tabs, the search and prominent tabs counted, and keep it visible in every section. Put actions that change the content of a section in a Toolbar instead. A desktop layout navigates with a Sidebar or Tabs, so the bar renders under 768px only.

## Examples

The example shows the phone view. Its settings change the bar in place.

The bar is a `nav` named Tabs. Each tab is a button, or a link with `href`, and the selected one has `aria-current="page"`; every tab is a stop for the Tab key. The search tab and the prominent tab are named by their label, and a badge is read after the label, as in Calendar 3. The accessory is read where it stands: before the tabs, and minimized between the tab and the search or prominent circle. Minimized, the selected tab that stays in the capsule has `aria-expanded="false"`: pressing it brings the other tabs back. When the search or the prominent tab is the selected one, the capsule keeps its first tab, and pressing that tab selects it.

## Styling

TabBar reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--tab-bar-gutter`, `--tab-bar-gap`: space at the sides and between the capsule and the search circle (default `21px`, `8px`)
- `--tab-bar-padding-top`, `--tab-bar-padding-bottom`: space above and below the capsule (default `12px`, `21px` or the safe area)
- `--tab-bar-minimized-padding-top`, `--tab-bar-minimized-gutter`, `--tab-bar-minimized-padding-bottom`: space around the minimized circles (default `19px`, `28px`, `28px` or the safe area)
- `--tab-bar-capsule-padding-block`, `--tab-bar-capsule-padding-inline`: the capsule around the tabs (default `4px`, `10px`)
- `--tab-bar-item-width`, `--tab-bar-item-height`, `--tab-bar-pill-outset`: the widest tab, its height and how far the pill reaches past it (default `85px`, `54px`, `6px`)
- `--tab-bar-icon-box`, `--tab-bar-icon-size`: the line of the symbol and the icon (default `28px`, `28px`)
- `--tab-bar-label-font-size`, `--tab-bar-label-line-height`, `--tab-bar-label-font-weight`: the label (default `10px`, `12px`, semibold)
- `--tab-bar-search-size`, `--tab-bar-minimized-size`: the search or prominent circle and the minimized circles (default `62px`, `48px`)
- `--tab-bar-item-color`, `--tab-bar-item-color-selected`, `--tab-bar-item-color-minimized`: tab colors (default `light-dark(var(--gray-750), var(--gray-850))`, `var(--color-text)`, `var(--color-text)`)
- `--tab-bar-item-background-selected`: the selection pill (default `var(--alpha-08)`)
- `--tab-bar-badge-size`, `--tab-bar-badge-padding-inline`, `--tab-bar-badge-font-size`, `--tab-bar-badge-font-weight`: the badge capsule and its text (default `18px`, `5px`, `12px`, normal)
- `--tab-bar-badge-top`, `--tab-bar-badge-start`: where the badge sits, below the top of the tab and past its middle (default `2.33px`, `11.67px`)
- `--tab-bar-minimized-badge-top`, `--tab-bar-minimized-badge-start`: the same on the minimized circle, from its top and its middle (default `2px`, `9.67px`)
- `--tab-bar-badge-background`, `--tab-bar-badge-color`: badge colors (default `var(--color-background-danger-solid)`, `var(--color-text-danger-solid)`)
- `--tab-bar-accessory-height`, `--tab-bar-accessory-gap`, `--tab-bar-minimized-accessory-gap`: the accessory, the space between it and the tabs, and minimized between it and the circles (default `48px`, `8px`, `13.33px`)
- The capsule, the circles and the accessory use the shared mobile glass: `--mobile-glass-background`, `--mobile-glass-sheen`, `--mobile-glass-shadow-raised` and `--mobile-glass-backdrop-filter`.
