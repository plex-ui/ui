Render `<Toaster />` once near the root of your app, then call `toast(...)` from any handler. PlexUI renders toasts and manages their interaction while [sonner](https://sonner.emilkowal.ski/) supplies the toast store. Its full API (`toast.promise`, `toast.loading`, `toast.dismiss`, `toast.custom`) is re-exported as-is.

PlexUI implements the compatible sonner `Toaster` options (`expand`, `visibleToasts`, `hotkey`, `dir`, `swipeDirections`, etc.) in its renderer. `theme`, `richColors`, `toastOptions`, `style` and `closeButton` are left out of `ToasterProps`, because Plex sets them for its tokens, icons and theme sync.

### toast

Re-exported from sonner. The full surface (`toast()`, `.success`, `.error`, `.warning`, `.info`, `.message`, `.loading`, `.promise`, `.dismiss`, `.custom`) is available with full TypeScript inference.

### Types

`Action`, `ExternalToast`, and `ToastT` are re-exported so you can type helpers without importing from `sonner` directly.

### Screen readers

The toast region is not a live region itself, so a toast is never read twice. A hidden polite announcer reads each toast once, its title and then its description, and again only when its words change, as a promise that settles or a custom toast that updates itself. An error that waits to be closed, `toast.error(...)` with `duration: Infinity` (its own or the Toaster's), is `role="alert"` instead: read at once, and only by its role. One that turns up behind the stack, where its role cannot speak, is read by a hidden assertive announcer instead.

### Over a modal Dialog

The region stays a layer of its own above a modal [Dialog](/components/dialog). The Dialog hides the rest of the page from a screen reader, but not the region, so its toasts are still read. Focus can move into a toast (Alt+T, or a press on its button) without the Dialog's focus trap pulling it back, Escape there returns focus to the Dialog without closing it, and a press on a toast's close or action button is not a press outside: the Dialog stays open. While a modal layer is open, the region keeps its presses and focus from the document listeners added after the Toaster mounted, so render `<Toaster />` once at the root, as above. A Toaster mounted later still keeps a PlexUI Dialog open on a press, but the Dialog's focus trap can then pull focus back.

### Theme sync

`<Toaster />` reads `data-theme` on `<html>` on mount and observes changes via `MutationObserver`. Toggling light ↔ dark rethemes toasts that are already on screen, with no `next-themes` dependency.

## Styling

Toast reads the shared semantic color, typography and spacing tokens listed in [Design tokens](/docs/foundations/design-tokens), so it follows theme changes made there. The close button (`closeButton` on a toast) stands at the trailing end of the toast, centred on a one-line message and level with the title beside a description, and draws a 20px face in the text color with the ring of an outline Button, visible in both themes, inside a 26px target; on a touch screen its target is 44px, at the trailing top corner. These variables move the toaster per page, set on `:root` or any ancestor of the Toaster (a page with its own top bar, say):

- `--toast-offset-top`, `--toast-offset-bottom`: distance from the top and bottom of the window above 600px (default `32px`)
- `--toast-mobile-offset-top`, `--toast-mobile-offset-bottom`: the same under 600px (default `16px` past the safe area)
- `--z-toast`: stack order of the toaster (default `100000`)
