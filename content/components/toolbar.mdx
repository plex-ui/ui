## Hosts

A toolbar places itself by its parent:

- In `Page`, the top bar covers the content below the device's top safe area, or the bar's gutter from the window's top where there is none (a page in a browser), and `Page.Content` starts below it. The bottom bar stays below the content, which scrolls under it. Under 768px a top toolbar replaces `Page.Header` and a bottom toolbar replaces `Page.Footer`, so one page can keep its desktop header and its phone toolbar side by side.
- In a `Dialog` Sheet, the top bar sits 16px under the top edge, hides the Sheet's own bar and Close button, and leaves `Dialog.Header` to name the dialog. Its title is hidden from assistive technology there. A bottom bar replaces `Dialog.Footer`.

Put the Back button of a phone screen in the toolbar only. A desktop layout shows its own navigation, such as a sidebar or breadcrumbs.

## API

### Toolbar

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `placement` | `"top" \| "bottom"` | `"top"` | Top bar with 44px targets, or bottom bar with 48px targets |
| `titleStyle` | `"inline" \| "large" \| "compact-large" \| "compact-medium"` | `"inline"` | Size and place of `Toolbar.Title` in a top bar |
| `titleAlign` | `"center" \| "start"` | `"center"` | Alignment of an inline title. Large and compact titles always start at the leading edge |
| `background` | `"soft" \| "hard" \| "glass" \| "blur"` | `"glass"` at the top, `"soft"` at the bottom | How the bar meets the content scrolled under it, see [Background](#background). `"edge"` is the earlier name of `"soft"` and `"solid"` of `"hard"` |
| `className` | `string` | — | Applied to the bar |

All other `<div>` attributes and `ref` are forwarded to the bar.

### Toolbar.Title

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `subtitle` | `ReactNode` | — | Second, smaller line below the title |
| `as` | `"h1" \| "h2" \| "h3"` | `"h1"` | Heading level of the title |

### Compound parts

| Part | Element | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| `Toolbar.Leading` | `<div>` | Back, Close or menu controls at the leading edge |
| `Toolbar.Title` | `<h1>` | Title of the screen, with an optional subtitle |
| `Toolbar.Content` | `<div>` | Middle content: a search field, a main action or a segmented control. `fill` stretches it across the row |
| `Toolbar.Group` | `<div role="group">` | Related buttons in one capsule |
| `Toolbar.Trailing` | `<div>` | Actions at the trailing edge |
| `Toolbar.Row` | `<div>` | A row under the controls, part of the bar: a segmented control or a search field across its width; several stand in their order. Under a large title on a page they stand under the title in the content and pin under the bar once scrolled up to it |

## Styling

Buttons, capsules and the search field in a toolbar use the shared mobile glass of iOS 26: a light fill over a frosted backdrop that turns the content under a control into a smudge, an outline that darkens whatever lies under it, a thin rim of light along the top and bottom edges and a sheen darker at the top and lighter at the bottom. At rest the glass casts no shadow; once content is under the bar, it does. Icons take a 24px box in both bars, labels are 17px medium. `NavigationButton` keeps its own glyph sizes.

Toolbar reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--toolbar-gutter`, `--toolbar-bottom-gutter`: side padding of the top and bottom bars (default `16px` and `28px`; a page's bottom bar on a band takes `--toolbar-gutter`)
- `--toolbar-top-item-size`, `--toolbar-bottom-item-size`: button and field height (default `44px` and `48px`)
- `--toolbar-top-item-gap`, `--toolbar-bottom-item-gap`: gap between items (default `10px` and `12px`)
- `--toolbar-group-padding`, `--toolbar-group-gap`: inner padding and spacing of a capsule in the top bar (default `1px` and `10px`, as iOS 27.2 sets two symbols in a 100px capsule; `2px` and `8px` in the bottom bar)
- `--toolbar-label-gutter`, `--toolbar-label-font-weight`, `--toolbar-label-prominent-font-weight`: space from a text button's edge to its label in the top bar and the label's weight, a solid button's apart (default `16px`, `510` and `590`, as iOS 27.2 sets Cancel and a prominent Done; `--toolbar-bottom-label-gutter` is the bottom bar's, also `16px`)
- `--toolbar-padding-bottom`: space below the top row (default `10px`)
- `--toolbar-row-gap`: space between the controls and a row under them, and between two rows (default `10px`)
- `--toolbar-sheet-padding-top`: space above the bar in a Sheet, which sets its controls 38px below the top edge as iOS 27.2 does (default `16px`)
- `--toolbar-bottom-padding-top`, `--toolbar-bottom-padding-bottom`: padding of the bottom bar (default `4px`, and the bottom safe area less `6px`, at least `16px`: the 48px items stand 28px above the screen's edge over the 34px home indicator inset, as in iOS 26 Mail and Contacts)
- `--mobile-glass-background`, `--mobile-glass-sheen`, `--mobile-glass-shadow`, `--mobile-glass-shadow-raised`, `--mobile-glass-backdrop-filter`: glass of the buttons, capsules and search field, at rest and with content under the bar
- `--toolbar-title-font-size`, `--toolbar-large-title-font-size`, `--toolbar-medium-title-font-size`: title sizes (default `17px`, `34px` and `22px`)
- `--toolbar-large-title-gap`: space between the row of controls and a large title (default `13.3px`: the capitals start 22px under the row, as on iOS 27.2); a subtitle goes under the title and never moves it
- `--toolbar-large-title-descent`, `--toolbar-large-subtitle-descent`: the part of the title's and the subtitle's line below the baseline (default `8.3px` and `4.6px`). On a page the title stands at the top of `Page.Content` and the content starts 16px under its last baseline; the bar and `--page-top` end under the row of controls
- `--toolbar-icon-size`, `--toolbar-icon-size-sm`, `--toolbar-search-icon-size`: icon boxes of the buttons, of a button with `iconSize="sm"` and of the search field (default `26px`, `23.56px` and `23.75px`). A symbol with less ink, as `PlusLg`, takes `iconSize="sm"`, and a search field takes `SearchSm`, so they draw the 17.67px plus and the 16.33px magnifier of iOS 26
- `--toolbar-search-gutter`, `--toolbar-search-icon-gap`: space before the magnifier and between it and the text (default `10.54px` and `6.43px`: the magnifier's ink starts 14px into the field and the text's ink at 41.67px)
- `--toolbar-search-font-weight`, `--toolbar-search-placeholder-color`: the search text in Regular (`400`) and its placeholder in the secondary label color (default `light-dark(var(--alpha-40), var(--alpha-60))`)
- `--toolbar-background`: color of the fading edge and of a hard or glass bar (defaults to the Sheet or page canvas)
- `--page-bar-hard-line`: hairline of a hard bar (default `var(--page-bar-line)`, as iOS 27.2 draws it)
- `--page-bar-blur-light`, `--page-bar-blur-heavy`: blurs of a blur bar, over the bar and past it, and toward the screen's edge (default `3px`, `12px`); `--page-bar-blur-top-mask`, `--page-bar-blur-top-heavy-mask` and their `-bottom-` pair place them
- `--page-bar-line`, `--page-bar-veil-opacity`: hairline and opacity of a glass bar (default `light-dark(rgb(0 0 0 / 10%), var(--color-border))` and `light-dark(72%, 60%)`)
- `--toolbar-prominent-disabled-background`, `--toolbar-prominent-disabled-color`: capsule and label of a disabled prominent action (default `light-dark(var(--alpha-12), var(--alpha-20))` and `light-dark(var(--gray-0), var(--alpha-30))`). A disabled glass button keeps the glass and takes `--mobile-label-tertiary`
