## Hosts

A toolbar places itself by its parent:

- In `Page`, the top bar covers the content below the device's top safe area, and `Page.Content` starts below it. The bottom bar stays below the content, which scrolls under it. Under 768px a top toolbar replaces `Page.Header` and a bottom toolbar replaces `Page.Footer`, so one page can keep its desktop header and its phone toolbar side by side.
- In a `Dialog` Sheet, the top bar starts with the grabber, hides the Sheet's own bar and Close button, and leaves `Dialog.Header` to name the dialog. Its title is hidden from assistive technology there. A bottom bar replaces `Dialog.Footer`.

Put the Back button of a phone screen in the toolbar only. A desktop layout shows its own navigation, such as a sidebar or breadcrumbs.

## API

### Toolbar

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `placement` | `"top" \| "bottom"` | `"top"` | Top bar with 44px targets, or bottom bar with 48px targets |
| `titleStyle` | `"inline" \| "large" \| "compact-large" \| "compact-medium"` | `"inline"` | Size and place of `Toolbar.Title` in a top bar |
| `titleAlign` | `"center" \| "start"` | `"center"` | Alignment of an inline title. Large and compact titles always start at the leading edge |
| `background` | `"soft" \| "hard" \| "glass" \| "solid"` | `"soft"` | How the bar meets the content scrolled under it, see [Background](#background). `"edge"` is the earlier name of `"soft"` |
| `className` | `string` | — | Applied to the bar |

All other `<div>` attributes and `ref` are forwarded to the bar.

### Toolbar.Title

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `subtitle` | `ReactNode` | — | Second, smaller line below the title |
| `as` | `"h1" \| "h2" \| "h3"` | `"h1"` | Heading level of the title |

### Compound parts

| Part | Element | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| `Toolbar.Leading` | `<div>` | Back, Close or menu controls at the leading edge |
| `Toolbar.Title` | `<h1>` | Title of the screen, with an optional subtitle |
| `Toolbar.Content` | `<div>` | Middle content: a search field, a main action or a segmented control. `fill` stretches it across the row |
| `Toolbar.Group` | `<div role="group">` | Related buttons in one capsule |
| `Toolbar.Trailing` | `<div>` | Actions at the trailing edge |

## Styling

Buttons, capsules and the search field in a toolbar use the shared mobile glass of iOS 26: a light fill over a softly frosted backdrop, a thin rim of light on the top-left and bottom-right edges and a sheen darker at the top and lighter at the bottom. At rest the glass casts no shadow; once content scrolls under the top bar, it does. Icons take a 24px box in both bars, labels are 17px medium. `NavigationButton` keeps its own glyph sizes.

Toolbar reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--toolbar-gutter`, `--toolbar-bottom-gutter`: side padding of the top and bottom bars (default `16px` and `28px`)
- `--toolbar-top-item-size`, `--toolbar-bottom-item-size`: button and field height (default `44px` and `48px`)
- `--toolbar-top-item-gap`, `--toolbar-bottom-item-gap`: gap between items (default `10px` and `12px`)
- `--toolbar-group-padding`, `--toolbar-group-gap`: inner padding and spacing of a capsule in the top bar (default `2px` and `12px`; `4px` and `6px` in the bottom bar)
- `--toolbar-padding-bottom`: space below the top row (default `10px`)
- `--toolbar-sheet-padding-top`: space for the grabber in a Sheet (default `16px`)
- `--toolbar-bottom-padding-top`, `--toolbar-bottom-padding-bottom`: padding of the bottom bar (default `4px`, and `16px` or the bottom safe area less `2px`)
- `--mobile-glass-background`, `--mobile-glass-sheen`, `--mobile-glass-shadow`, `--mobile-glass-shadow-raised`, `--mobile-glass-backdrop-filter`: glass of the buttons, capsules and search field, at rest and with content under the bar
- `--toolbar-title-font-size`, `--toolbar-large-title-font-size`, `--toolbar-medium-title-font-size`: title sizes (default `17px`, `34px` and `22px`)
- `--toolbar-large-title-gap`: space between the row of controls and a large title (default `21px`)
- `--toolbar-icon-size`, `--toolbar-search-icon-size`: icon boxes of the buttons and of the search field (default `24px` and `26px`)
- `--toolbar-background`: color of the fading edge and of a hard, glass or solid bar (defaults to the Sheet or page canvas)
- `--page-bar-hard-line`: line of a hard bar (default `light-dark(rgb(0 0 0 / 12%), var(--color-border))`, the iOS 26 kit's Hard edge)
- `--page-bar-line`, `--page-bar-veil-opacity`: hairline and opacity of a glass bar (default `light-dark(rgb(0 0 0 / 10%), var(--color-border))` and `light-dark(72%, 60%)`)
- `--toolbar-prominent-disabled-background`, `--toolbar-prominent-disabled-color`: capsule and label of a disabled prominent action (default `light-dark(var(--alpha-12), var(--alpha-20))` and `light-dark(var(--gray-0), var(--alpha-30))`). A disabled glass button keeps the glass and takes `--mobile-label-tertiary`
