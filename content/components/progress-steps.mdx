### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<><code>current</code> is the active step, counted from 1: the steps before it are completed and show a check. <code>variant="minimal"</code> draws a compact bar of segments instead, one per step, and takes the count as <code>total</code>; it has no orientation, connector, icons or text, so those settings are for the default variant, and it draws <code>sm</code> as <code>md</code>, so it offers <code>md</code> and <code>lg</code>. <code>orientation="vertical"</code> stacks the steps, for a sidebar or a multi-step form. <code>size</code> sets the scale of the indicators, and <code>color</code> the color scheme of completed and active steps. <code>connectorStyle</code> draws the lines ahead of the current step solid or dashed; a completed line is solid, so at the last step the setting is not offered. <code>icon</code> on a <code>ProgressSteps.Step</code> shows a custom icon instead of its number. A step shows a <code>Title</code> and a <code>Description</code>; leave out the description, or show the indicators alone when the step's details are shown elsewhere. Keep the title then for screen readers with <code>visuallyHidden</code>: an icon or a check has no name, and the step takes no room under its indicator.<br /><br />On a phone the four columns of a horizontal row cannot hold a step's description, so the steps stand vertically there and the phone has no <code>orientation</code> setting: <code>useBreakpoint("md")</code> tells which to use. Progress steps have no iOS control of their own; With Navigation shows a compact composition for a phone screen.</>} code={overviewCode} previewMinHeight={168}>
