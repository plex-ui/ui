A toggle group sets several on or off choices that belong together, where any number may be on, such as the days an alarm repeats on. Each item is a Button: solid in the group's color while pressed, soft secondary otherwise. For one choice out of several, use a SegmentedControl or a RadioGroup; for a single setting, a Switch or a Checkbox.

## Examples

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>Round items spread across their row are the weekday buttons of the iOS Clock app's Repeat row, as in Add Alarm. On a phone with <code>touch.css</code> each is a 35pt circle with its letter at 17pt Semibold, as the native ones are. A narrow letter alone does not name a day: give each item the day's full name with <code>aria-label</code>.     new Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-US', { weekday, timeZone: 'UTC' }).format(date)

The group is a toolbar with one Tab stop. Tab enters it on the first pressed item, else on the item focused last, else on the first, and the arrow keys move between items in the reading direction of `dir`, looping at the ends. Space and Enter press or release the focused item. Each item is a button with `aria-pressed`.

## Styling

ToggleGroup reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it; the items read the Button tokens:

- `--toggle-group-gap`: space between items without `block` (default `8px`)
- `--toggle-group-disabled-opacity`: opacity of a disabled item, pressed or not (default `0.5`)
- `--toggle-group-round-size`, `--toggle-group-round-font-size`: the circle of a `pill` `uniform` item and its letter at 767px and below with `touch.css` (`35px`, `17px`, the weekday buttons of the iOS Clock app)
