A stepper changes a value by small amounts and shows no value of its own. Show the value beside it and name the stepper with the label of that value. For values that vary widely, pair it with an input. On a phone it is the 92 × 32 capsule of the iOS 26 kit with a 44px target; on a desktop it follows the PlexUI control size.

The stepper is a spin button with the current value, range and name. Its two segments are buttons that a pointer or assistive technology presses; they take no keyboard focus, and pressing one keeps the focus where it was. With the keyboard, Up and Down step, Page Up and Page Down step ten times, Home and End go to `min` and `max`.

## Styling

Stepper reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--stepper-height`: height (default `var(--control-size-md)`, `32px` at 767px and below)
- `--stepper-segment-width`: width of each segment (default `40px`, `46px` at 767px and below)
- `--stepper-radius`: corner radius with `pill={false}` (default `var(--control-radius-md)`)
- `--stepper-background`: fill (default `light-dark(var(--gray-100), var(--alpha-08))`, `--stepper-mobile-background` at 767px and below: `--alpha-05` and `--alpha-08`, the kit fill)
- `--stepper-segment-background-hover`, `--stepper-segment-background-active`: fill a segment adds on hover and while pressed (`--alpha-05`, `--alpha-10`; on a phone no hover and `--alpha-08`)
- `--stepper-separator-color`, `--stepper-separator-inset`: separator color and its inset from the top and bottom (default `var(--color-border)`, `4px`; `--alpha-25` at 767px and below)
- `--stepper-icon-size`, `--stepper-icon-color`, `--stepper-icon-disabled-color`: the minus and plus (default `var(--control-icon-size-md)`, `var(--color-text)`, `var(--color-text-disabled)`; `22px` and the tertiary label at 767px and below)
- `--stepper-target-size`: height of the touch target (`44px` at 767px and below)
