Swipe actions keep the actions of a row out of sight until they are wanted, as the swipe actions of an iOS list do: a swipe toward the leading edge reveals the trailing actions, a swipe toward the trailing edge the leading ones, and a full swipe runs the first action of that side. Give a row the actions people take on it often, such as Delete or Archive, and keep the same actions in reach elsewhere too, as in a menu, for people who never swipe.

## Examples

The examples show the phone view.

### SwipeAction

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "id", type: "string", description: "Unique ID of the action within its side" },

A swipe starts once the finger has gone 10px across the row and farther across than down; a drag down scrolls the list, and a drag in a text field or along a slider stays with that control. A click that ends a swipe, or that closes an open row, does not reach the content. Each action is also a button, in the order of the screen: the leading actions before the content, the trailing ones after it. The focus on an action reveals its side, and the row closes when the focus leaves it; an action chosen from the keys keeps the focus, so its side stays open. The row has `data-revealed` with the side it shows, and `data-swiping` while a finger moves it.

## Styling

SwipeActions reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--swipe-actions-gap`, `--swipe-actions-edge-inset`, `--swipe-actions-content-gap`, `--swipe-actions-inset-block`: the space between the actions, from the edge, from the content, and inside the top and bottom of the row (default `10px`, `10px`, `10px`, `4px`)
- `--swipe-actions-action-min-width`, `--swipe-actions-action-padding-inline`, `--swipe-actions-icon-size`, `--swipe-actions-circle-size`: a capsule with its symbol only, and the circle it becomes (default `60px`, `8px`, `20px`, `50px`)
- `--swipe-actions-labeled-padding-inline`, `--swipe-actions-labeled-icon-size`, `--swipe-actions-label-gap`: a capsule with its label inside (default `16px`, `18px`, `6.67px`)
- `--swipe-actions-text-padding-inline`, `--swipe-actions-text-radius`: an action without an icon (default `16px`, `16px`)
- `--swipe-actions-label-font-size`, `--swipe-actions-label-line-height`, `--swipe-actions-label-font-weight`: the labels (default `13px`, `16px`, normal)
- `--swipe-actions-tall-padding-top`, `--swipe-actions-tall-padding-bottom`, `--swipe-actions-caption-gap`, `--swipe-actions-caption-font-weight`, `--swipe-actions-caption-color`: a capsule with its label below (default `4.33px`, `3.67px`, `4px`, normal, the secondary text color)
- `--swipe-actions-background`, `--swipe-actions-content-background`: behind a revealed row and its content, unset by default (on a phone the canvas of the Page or Dialog and the soft gray background, on a desktop the secondary surface and the surface)
- `--swipe-actions-content-radius`: the rounded side of the content (default `26px`)
- `--swipe-actions-duration`: how long the row takes to open or close (default `250ms`)
