**Menu.Item**: List item. Use `onSelect` or `onClick` for actions; without either the item is non-interactive content.

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "description", type: "ReactNode", description: "Second line under the label. The label turns semibold on its own" },
  { prop: "onSelect", type: "(event: Event) => void", description: "Called when item is selected; call event.preventDefault() to keep the menu open" },
  { prop: "onClick", type: "(event: MouseEvent) => void", description: "Called when the item is clicked; also makes the item interactive" },
  { prop: "disabled", type: "boolean", default: "false", description: "Disable the item" },
  { prop: "danger", type: "boolean", default: "false", description: "Destructive action: the item is drawn in the danger color" },
  { prop: "className", type: "string", description: "Applied to the item" },
]} />

**Menu.Link**: Item that navigates. Internal paths render the link component from `PlexUIProvider`; absolute `http(s)` URLs open in a new tab. Pass `href` or `to`, `disabled` (a disabled link does not navigate), `as` and link props; `danger` marks a destructive destination.

**Menu.Separator**: Divider between items. No props.

**Menu.Group**: Groups related items. Wraps `Menu.Item`, `Menu.Link`, or `Menu.Label`. No specific props.

**Menu.Label**: Non-interactive label for a group. Accepts `className`.

**Menu.Header**: Names what the menu acts on, above its items. The children are the title.

## Styling

With the optional `touch.css` stylesheet, phone menus use 34px corners (the iOS 27 kit Menu), 17px labels with 20px line height, and 13/18px subtitles. Rows are 42px, as in native iOS 26 menus, and long labels wrap and make a row taller. A pressed row is the capsule iOS draws under the finger: the whole row, 10px from the panel's edges, round at its ends, in one even gray (`--menu-press-background`). A submenu grows out of the row that opened it while the menu behind it steps back. A checked item keeps its checkmark without a persistent background, centered 28px from the panel's edge with labels at 43.5px, as native menus draw it; in a menu with checkmarks, plain rows and section titles start where the labels do. A menu and a submenu are 250px wide, as native iOS menus are, so a long label wraps under itself; an explicit width or minimum width still takes precedence, and a submenu of `auto` width fits its content.

On a phone a menu without `sideOffset` grows out of its trigger and covers it, as iOS menus do: its top is the trigger's top, or its bottom when it opens upward, and its aligned edge is the trigger's edge. Without `side` it opens toward the larger part of the screen, upward from a trigger in the lower half even when it would fit below. A desktop keeps the 5px gap below the trigger.

On phones, menus and submenus inside a Page or Dialog keep clear of its visible footer. Their available space updates when the footer or viewport changes.

A phone here is a window 767px wide or narrower, or the inside of a [PhoneLayout](/components/phone-layout) or a [Dialog](/components/dialog#api) with `layout="phone"`, at any window width. A menu opened in a PhoneLayout opens in its box and keeps inside it; one opened in a phone Dialog opens over the window, as on a phone.

In the dark theme a phone menu is glass over the page, as native iOS 26 menus are: white at 12% over an 18px blur of what is under it, which leaves the page's text unreadable through the panel (a 37 by 24pt switch knob behind a native menu spreads to 55 by 37pt). The light theme keeps the elevated surface.

Menu reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--menu-checkbox-indicator-size`: checkbox indicator size (default `var(--font-text-lg-size)`)
- `--menu-font-size`: font size (default `var(--font-text-sm-size)`)
- `--menu-gutter`: gutter (default `spacing(1.5)`)
- `--menu-item-background-color`: item background color (default `light-dark(var(--alpha-08), var(--alpha-10))`)
- `--menu-mobile-background`: panel surface on a phone (default the elevated surface in light, `var(--alpha-12)` glass in dark)
- `--menu-mobile-backdrop-filter`: blur behind the panel on a phone in the dark theme (default `blur(18px)`)
- `--menu-default-width`: default width before a content-level override (none on desktop, 250px with touch styles)
- `--menu-default-min-width`: default minimum width before a content-level override (300px on desktop, 250px with touch styles)
- `--menu-horizontal-min-width`: minimum width of a horizontal menu (none on desktop, 250px with touch styles)
- `--menu-item-press-scale`: pressed surface scale (1 with touch styles; otherwise follows the pointer interaction scale)
- `--menu-item-gap`: item gap (default `spacing(1.5)`)
- `--menu-item-padding`: item padding (default `spacing(1.5) spacing(2)`)
- `--menu-line-height`: line height (default `var(--font-text-sm-line-height)`)
- `--menu-radio-indicator-hole-size`: radio indicator hole size (default `var(--font-text-3xs-size)`)
- `--menu-radio-indicator-size`: radio indicator size (default `var(--font-text-lg-size)`)
- `--menu-radius`: radius (default `var(--radius-xl)`; `34px` on a phone with `touch.css`, the iOS 27 kit Menu)
- `--menu-separator-background-color`: separator background color (default `var(--color-border)`)
- `--menu-separator-gutter`: separator gutter (default `var(--menu-gutter) calc(-1 * var(--menu-gutter))`)
- `--radio-group-indicator-background-color`: indicator background color (default `var(--color-background-primary-solid)`)
- `--radio-group-indicator-border-color`: indicator border color (default `var(--color-border-primary-outline)`)
- `--radio-group-indicator-border-color-hover`: indicator border color hover (default `var(--alpha-25)`)
- `--radio-group-indicator-hole-background-color`: indicator hole background color (default `var(--color-text-primary-solid)`)
