const [disabled, setDisabled] = useState(false)
const [monthDisabled, setMonthDisabled] = useState(false)
const [showBadges, setShowBadges] = useState(false)

// Each view's events, read with its name and drawn with showBadges

const views = [

  { value: "week", label: "Week", icon: <CalendarToday /> },

  { value: "year", label: "Year", icon: <Grid /> },

const control = (
  <SegmentedControl
    aria-label="Calendar view"
    value={tab}
    onChange={setTab}
    size={size}

    mobileSegmentWidth={mobileSegmentWidth}
    disabled={disabled}

        badge={EVENTS[view.value]}
      >
        {icon === "only" ? undefined : view.label}
      </SegmentedControl.Tab>
    ))}
  </SegmentedControl>
)

<>

    <Toolbar>
      <Toolbar.Content>{control}</Toolbar.Content>

    </List>
  </ScrollView>
  {placement === "bottom" && (
    <Toolbar placement="bottom">
      <Toolbar.Content>{control}</Toolbar.Content>
    </Toolbar>
  )}
</>`

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>The props follow <code>@plexui/ui</code>'s SegmentedControl, with <code>SegmentedControl.Tab</code> for each segment (<code>SegmentedControl.Option</code> is the same part).</p>
  <p>On iOS horizontal tabs are the iOS 26 segmented control, a capsule whose thumb slides to the selection, in its two sizes. The default <code>md</code> and larger are the large control of iOS 26 apps, as Calendar and Reminders draw it and SwiftUI draws a segmented Picker in a toolbar: a 48pt glass capsule, a 40pt gray selection 4pt inside it, 8pt between segments, every label 15pt Medium. <code>sm</code> and smaller are the small one, the plain control of SwiftUI's Picker in content: a 32pt capsule of the tertiary fill, a white selection 2pt inside it, labels 13pt, Semibold when selected. <code>mobileMaterial</code> switches the look, as for Button: <code>flat</code> makes the large one plain, 49pt as SwiftUI's <code>.controlSize(.large)</code>, and <code>glass</code> puts the small one under the Liquid Glass of iOS 26. On Android the control is a capsule of the bar's surface (white in light mode, a gray in dark mode) with a soft shadow, 36dp at <code>md</code> (32 at <code>sm</code>, 40 at <code>lg</code>), its segments 4dp in, 14sp Medium labels in the secondary gray, the selected one in the accent on a pill of the accent at 12%. The pill slides to a new selection in 320ms, and the labels' colors blend with it. In a bar's row of controls it takes the 46dp height of the circles beside it.</p>

The capsule grows with a larger text size. The example counts each view's events.</p>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Segmented control | A segmented capsule, in place of Material's connected button group |
| Look | A capsule whose thumb slides to the selection | A white capsule, the selected segment on a sliding pill of the accent at 12% |

Not Material's Tabs, which sit under a top app bar with an underline.
