The props follow `@plexui/ui`'s SegmentedControl, with `SegmentedControl.Tab` for each segment (`SegmentedControl.Option` is the same part). On iOS the control is a capsule whose thumb slides to the selection: at `md` and larger the 48pt glass capsule of iOS 26 apps, at `sm` and smaller the 32pt plain capsule of SwiftUI's segmented Picker. On Android it is Material 3 Expressive's connected button group, the successor of segmented buttons: 40dp buttons 2dp apart, the outer corners round and the inner ones 8dp, the selected one fully round in the Plex primary (the ink, or a brand's), as a selected Button.

### Sizes

iOS has two controls: `sm` and smaller the small plain one, `md` and larger the large glass one. `mobileMaterial="flat"` gives the large one the plain look of SwiftUI's `.controlSize(.large)` (49pt), and `"glass"` gives the small one glass. Android draws the same 40dp group at every size.

<RNPreview demo="segmented-control/Sizes" code={sizesCode} />

### Widths

Segments are as wide as the widest one by default, as UISegmentedControl draws them. `mobileSegmentWidth="content"` gives each segment its own label's width, and `block` fills the row, the segments sharing its width.

<RNPreview demo="segmented-control/Widths" code={widthsCode} />

### Symbols

A segment takes an `icon`. A symbol alone needs an `aria-label`. With both a symbol and a label, iOS shows the symbol alone, as UISegmentedControl does, and Android shows both.

<RNPreview demo="segmented-control/Symbols" code={symbolsCode} />

### Disabled

`disabled` on a segment disables it alone; on the control, every segment.

<RNPreview demo="segmented-control/Disabled" code={disabledCode} />

A `badge` on a segment is not drawn on either platform, as neither iOS nor Material draws counts on segments: it is read with the segment's name ("Year, 2").
