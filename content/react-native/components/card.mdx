The parts follow `@plexui/ui`'s Card (`Card.Header`, `Card.Title`, `Card.Description`, `Card.Content`, `Card.Footer`, `Card.Action`), plus `Card.Media` for a picture. On iOS the phone layer's card: 26pt corners, 16pt padding, the title 17/22 semibold and the description 15/20 in the secondary label; an outline card is a section of an inset grouped list, so it stands on the grouped background. On Android Material 3's cards: 12dp corners, 16dp padding, the title in Title M and the description in Body M, an outlined card on `surface` with an `outlineVariant` border, a filled one on `surfaceContainerHighest`, the footer's buttons at the end.

### Variants

`outline` is a grouped section on iOS and an outlined card on Android; `solid` takes a gray fill on iOS and Material's filled card on Android; `ghost` has no fill.

<RNPreview demo="card/Variants" grouped code={variantsCode} />

### Sizes

`size` sets the padding: 12, 16 or 20 on iOS; 12, 16 or 24 on Android.

<RNPreview demo="card/Sizes" grouped code={sizesCode} />

### Media

`Card.Media` shows a picture. Placed first or last, it reaches the card's edges. `title` and `description` set a white caption over a gradient that darkens the bottom of the picture.

<RNPreview demo="card/Media" grouped code={mediaCode} />

### As a button

`onPress` makes the whole card one button, with the platform's pressed layer. A card whose own parts take the presses can show the press with `interactive` instead.

### Compound parts

| Part | Props | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| `Card.Header` | `children`, `style`, `testID` | The title and description, with a `Card.Action` at the end |
| `Card.Title` | `children` | The card's heading, announced as a header |
| `Card.Description` | `children` | Secondary text under the title |
| `Card.Content` | `children`, `style`, `testID` | The body of the card |
| `Card.Footer` | `children`, `style`, `testID` | The buttons at the bottom, at the end on Android |
| `Card.Action` | `children`, `style`, `testID` | An action at the end of the header, such as a menu or a close button |
