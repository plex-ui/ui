### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>The parts follow <code>@plexui/ui</code>'s Card (<code>Card.Header</code>, <code>Card.Title</code>, <code>Card.Description</code>, <code>Card.Content</code>, <code>Card.Footer</code>, <code>Card.Action</code>), plus <code>Card.Media</code> for a picture. On iOS the phone layer's card: 26pt corners, 16pt padding, the title 17/22 semibold and the description 15/20 in the secondary label; an outline card is a section of an inset grouped list, so it stands on the grouped background. On Android a card is the section card of a settings screen: 16dp corners and no border, the card's color on the gray page, 16dp padding, the title in Title M and the description in Body M, the footer's buttons at the end.</p>
  <p><code>variant</code> sets the frame: <code>outline</code> (the default), <code>solid</code> or <code>ghost</code> (no fill and no padding, its parts at its edges). On iOS an outline card is a section of an inset grouped list, as a List is, on the grouped background, and a solid card takes a gray fill to stand apart from those sections. On Android <code>outline</code> is that white section card, as a List&apos;s, and <code>solid</code> a gray fill (<code>surfaceContainerHigh</code>) with no border, to stand apart from it. <code>size</code> sets the padding: 12, 16 or 20 on iOS; 12, 16 or 24 on Android.</p>
  <p>A card is its parts: <code>Card.Header</code> with <code>Card.Title</code>, <code>Card.Description</code> and <code>Card.Action</code> (a slot at the top end), <code>Card.Content</code> and <code>Card.Footer</code> for the actions, at the end on Android. With <code>onPress</code> the whole card is one button, with the platform's pressed layer (on Android a List row's); it then holds no buttons of its own. A card whose own parts take the presses can show the press with <code>interactive</code> instead. <code>Card.Media</code> adds a picture that reaches the card's edges where it stands first or last, with an optional caption over it.</p>

### Compound parts

| Part | Props | Purpose |
|------|-------|---------|
| `Card.Header` | `children`, `style`, `testID` | The title and description, with a `Card.Action` at the end |
| `Card.Title` | `children` | The card's heading, announced as a header |
| `Card.Description` | `children` | Secondary text under the title |
| `Card.Content` | `children`, `style`, `testID` | The body of the card |
| `Card.Footer` | `children`, `style`, `testID` | The buttons at the bottom, at the end on Android |
| `Card.Action` | `children`, `style`, `testID` | An action at the end of the header, such as a menu or a close button |
