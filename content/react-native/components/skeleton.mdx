### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>The props follow <code>@plexui/ui</code>'s Skeleton. On iOS it is drawn from the Plex tokens: a block of <code>--skeleton-background</code> with <code>--skeleton-radius</code> corners that pulses between full and half opacity every 1.5 seconds. On Android it is a shimmering placeholder: a block of the text color at 6% (white at 5% in dark mode) with 4dp corners, and one light band, 600dp tall, that moves down the screen across every block at once, about every 1.4 seconds on a phone, so the blocks light as one gradient. On both it stands still when the system asks for reduced motion. Skeletons are hidden from screen readers: say what loads on their container with <code>{"accessibilityState={{ busy: true }}"}</code> and a label.</p>
  <p>A skeleton stands in for content while it loads, in its shape: compose skeletons as the content is laid out, and set each one's size with <code>width</code> and <code>height</code>, in points or as a percentage (without <code>width</code> it takes the whole width). <code>content</code> shows the placeholders of a profile, a card with a picture, a paragraph (lines of varying widths read as natural text), the rows of a list (an avatar and two lines each) and a post in a feed (its author, its text and a picture). A larger radius, as for the pictures of the card and the post, goes in <code>style</code>.</p>
  <p><code>circle</code> rounds a skeleton, for the avatar of a profile, a list row or a post; without it a skeleton keeps its own radius, as for a square picture. The skeletons are hidden from screen readers, so their container says what loads.</p>
</>} code={`import { Skeleton, useTheme } from "@plexui/react-native"

const theme = useTheme()
const roundedLg = { borderRadius: theme.length("--radius-lg") }

<View accessible accessibilityLabel="Loading" accessibilityState={{ busy: true }}>
[[#content=profile]]
  <View style={{ flexDirection: "row", alignItems: "center", gap: 16 }}>
    <Skeleton[[#circle]] circle[[/circle]] width={48} height={48} />
    <View style={{ gap: 8 }}>
      <Skeleton width={200} height={16} />
      <Skeleton width={150} height={16} />
    </View>
  </View>
[[/content=profile]]
[[#content=card]]
  <View style={{ width: 300, gap: 12, padding: 16, borderWidth: 1, borderColor: theme.color("--color-border"), borderRadius: theme.length("--radius-xl") }}>
    <Skeleton height={150} style={roundedLg} />
    <Skeleton width="75%" height={16} />
    <Skeleton width="50%" height={16} />
  </View>
[[/content=card]]
[[#content=text]]
  <View style={{ width: 300, gap: 8 }}>
    <Skeleton height={16} />
    <Skeleton height={16} />
    <Skeleton width="80%" height={16} />
  </View>
[[/content=text]]
[[#content=list]]
  {/* Rows of a list, as a chat list loads: an avatar and two lines */}
  <View style={{ width: 300 }}>
    {Array.from({ length: 3 }, (_, i) => (
      <View key={i} style={{ flexDirection: "row", alignItems: "center", gap: 12, paddingVertical: 12 }}>
        <Skeleton circle width={40} height={40} />
        <View style={{ flex: 1, gap: 8 }}>
          <Skeleton width="60%" height={16} />
          <Skeleton width="80%" height={12} />
        </View>
      </View>
    ))}
  </View>
[[/content=list]]
[[#content=feed]]
  {/* A post in a feed: its author, its text and a picture */}
  <View style={{ width: 300, gap: 12, padding: 16, borderWidth: 1, borderColor: theme.color("--color-border"), borderRadius: theme.length("--radius-xl") }}>
    <View style={{ flexDirection: "row", alignItems: "center", gap: 12 }}>
      <Skeleton circle width={32} height={32} />
      <View style={{ gap: 6 }}>
        <Skeleton width={120} height={14} />
        <Skeleton width={72} height={12} />
      </View>
    </View>
    <Skeleton height={16} />
    <Skeleton width="80%" height={16} />
    <Skeleton height={150} style={roundedLg} />
  </View>
[[/content=feed]]
</View>`} codeValues={{ content: "profile", circle: true }} previewMinHeight={168}>
  <RNExample demo="skeleton/Overview" />
</ComponentPreview>
