## Examples

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>IntroHeader opens a step of a sheet or a setup flow: what the step is for, before the field, the rows or the choices it asks for. The example is a step after iOS 27's Add Email Address. Use it once at the top of a step; a screen people come back to takes a bar title instead (<a href="/react-native/components/toolbar">Toolbar</a>), and a screen with nothing to show an <a href="/react-native/components/empty-message">Empty Message</a>.</p>
  <p>On iOS it is set as Apple's own steps set it (Apple Account, Add Email Address, Recovery Key, Pair Hearing Device, Set Up eSIM, Health's medication steps), measured on a 430pt phone: <code>media</code>, a symbol in the middle whose drawing is about 70pt (a kit icon takes 80pt and the accent), its bottom about 31pt over the title's line; the <code>title</code> in Title 2 bold (22/28); the <code>description</code>, optional, right under it in Title 2 in the secondary label. Both stand at the start of the column. On Android it is the setup wizard's header: a 40dp symbol in the primary at the start, 16 over a Headline Small title, the description in Body Large 16 under it.</p>
  <p><code>align</code> sets what the text lines up with. <code>cells</code>, the default, keeps it on the column of the content under it, the edge of the fields, cards and buttons, as a step with a field or a few rows; <code>text</code> moves it in by the row inset (20pt, 16dp), onto the text inside the cells. The header keeps no space round it: set the symbol's drawing about 24 under the bar's buttons (the bar keeps 10 under them, a symbol's box about 7 round its drawing) and the content 24 under the header.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} previewMinHeight={168}>
