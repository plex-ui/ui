## Examples

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>BottomActions holds a screen's main action at its bottom: an onboarding's Continue, a form's Create account. Use it for the one action that moves a person on from the screen, with at most a second, quieter one under it (a ghost Not now); actions of the content stay in the content. It is React Native only: the web has no bottom of a screen to stand at.</p>
  <p>On iOS the action is a capsule across the screen, 36pt from its sides and 52pt tall, its bottom just over the home indicator (2pt above the safe area), as iOS 26's own setup screens set it. On Android it is a full-width 48dp button, 16dp from the sides and 16dp above the gesture bar. Each Button inside is a block of the size <code>3xl</code> unless it sets its own <code>block</code> or <code>size</code>; a second action stands under the first, 8 apart.</p>
  <p><code>floating</code> lays the actions over the content, a scroll view under them, which then keeps their room at its end so its last line scrolls clear of them. Without it the actions take their own room at the end of the screen.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} previewMinHeight={168}>
