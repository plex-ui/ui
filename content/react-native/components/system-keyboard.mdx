<UsageBlock>{`import { SystemKeyboard } from "@plexui/react-native"`}</UsageBlock>

On a phone the system brings up its own keyboard; a web preview of the app on a computer has none, so a form there shows the screen as it never stands on the phone. `SystemKeyboard` draws the keyboard the phone would show, at the bottom of the window and as wide as it, while a field has focus. Its keys do not type: the reader types with their own keyboard.

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native | The system keyboard | Gboard |
| Drawn | 335pt with its suggestion bar (308 for a phone pad), keys of 43 every 54, 6.5 from the sides and 6 apart | 336dp with its row of tools and the 24dp navigation bar (292 for a number pad), keys of 47 every 59, 4.57 from the sides and 5.33 apart |
| On the device | Draws nothing: the system's keyboard comes up | Draws nothing: the system's keyboard comes up |

Every key stands within 1pt of the native keyboard's, measured on the iOS 26.2 simulator (iPhone 17 Pro) and a Pixel 9 emulator with Gboard; the keys widen with the screen and the margins stay. The glyphs are the kit's icons, in Inter.

## API

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "mode", type: '"auto" | "hidden"', default: '"auto"', description: "auto: drawn while a field has focus. hidden: never drawn, nor any other on the page (a still of a form as it stands)" },
  { prop: "field", type: "{ kind?: KeyboardKind; returnKey?: KeyboardReturnKey; caps?: boolean; autocorrect?: boolean }", description: "Draws the keyboard for this field with no field focused, still, at the foot of its own box: it takes neither the page's keyboard nor its inset. kind: text, email, url, search, password, number, decimal, phone. returnKey: default, go, search, send, next, done, join, route, previous" },
]} />

Nothing is drawn on a touch screen, whose browser brings up its own keyboard, nor on iOS and Android themselves. One keyboard draws on a page however many are mounted, on the page's body over the app and its sheets; a hidden one keeps the others from drawing. `keyboardLayout()` gives the keys' frames for a platform, a field and a width, and `KeyboardKeys` draws them where a picture of the keyboard is wanted.
