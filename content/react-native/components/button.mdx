<UsageBlock>{`import { Button, IconButton } from "@plexui/react-native"`}</UsageBlock>

The props follow `@plexui/ui`'s Button. On iOS a button takes the native heights of the phone layer (small 28, medium 34, large 50); on Android Material 3's sizes (XS 32 up to `sm`, S 40 at `md` and `lg`, M 56 from `xl`), with a 48dp touch target, the press's state layer and a shape that squares while pressed.
