### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>One button with every setting. <code>color</code> and <code>variant</code> style it: eight semantic colors, each <code>solid</code>, <code>soft</code>, <code>outline</code> or <code>ghost</code>. On iOS a button is an iOS 26 capsule: <code>solid</code> is Filled, <code>soft</code> Tinted, or Gray in <code>secondary</code>, and <code>ghost</code> Plain. iOS has only Filled, Tinted, Gray and Plain buttons, no outlined one, so on iOS <code>outline</code> is drawn as the Gray button: the neutral soft fill with the label in the button's color. On Android it is a pill with a 14sp Medium label at every size in the same Plex colors: <code>solid</code> is filled, <code>soft</code> tinted, <code>outline</code> outlined and <code>ghost</code> a text button. The example offers the colors of phone buttons: accent (<code>primary</code>), gray (<code>secondary</code>) and destructive (<code>danger</code>); the others are the semantic colors of the tokens.</p>
  <p><code>size</code> is the Plex UI scale, mapped to the platform's heights. iOS takes the three heights of its buttons: small 28 (<code>3xs</code> to <code>sm</code>), medium 34 (<code>md</code>, <code>lg</code>) and large 50 (<code>xl</code> to <code>3xl</code>). Android takes three steps around its big 48dp button: 32 up to <code>sm</code>, 40 at <code>md</code> and <code>lg</code>, and the big 48 from <code>xl</code>, each with a 48dp touch target. So the example offers <code>sm</code>, <code>md</code> and <code>xl</code>. A screen's main action is the large button, <code>xl</code> with <code>block</code>.</p>

On iOS <code>mobileMaterial="glass"</code> draws a soft or ghost button in the glass of iOS 26, and <code>mobileMaterial="clear"</code> in its clear glass, for a button over a photo, a video or a color: a light frost, a 0.5pt rim and the kit's #f5f5f5 label, the same in light and dark (Android, with no glass, gives it the gray at 17% and the same label); in a Toolbar a neutral soft or ghost button is glass by default. For an icon alone <code>uniform</code> makes the button as wide as it is tall; IconButton below is that button.</p>

## NavigationButton

## Platforms

| Plex | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Button | Button: a capsule, filled, gray or glass | Button: a pill, filled, tinted, outlined or text |
| IconButton | A circular button | A circular button; in a bar a 46dp glass circle |
| NavigationButton | Bar button item: the back chevron, close cross and confirm checkmark in glass circles | The back arrow and close cross in 46dp glass circles; confirm a glass text pill (an accent check circle in a bottom bar) |

iOS's button styles are Filled, Tinted, Gray and Plain, with no outlined button: on iOS `variant="outline"` is the Gray button, the neutral soft fill with the label in the button's color, while Android draws the outline.

`IconButton` takes the same props without `children`, `uniform` and `block`, plus `icon` (the icon element) and a required `accessibilityLabel`; its variant defaults to `"ghost"`.

`NavigationButton` takes them without `children`, `uniform` and `pill`, plus `action` (`"back" | "close" | "confirm"`, required), `accessibilityLabel` (default Back, Close or Confirm) and `label`: the short word on Android's confirm pill, Done by default (the pill never shows the `accessibilityLabel`); unused on iOS.
