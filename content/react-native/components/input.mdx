<UsageBlock>{`import { Input, TextField } from "@plexui/react-native"`}</UsageBlock>

The props follow `@plexui/ui`'s Input, on top of React Native's `TextInput`. On iOS a field takes the height of every phone control, with 17pt type and the radius of the control scale; `outline` draws a border that darkens on focus, `soft` a gray fill with no focus ring. On Android a field is Material 3's 56dp text field in Body L: `outline` is the outlined text field, `soft` the filled one (a `surfaceContainerHighest` fill over an indicator line) and `ghost` the filled one with no fill or line; the line turns 2dp in the accent on focus and the caret takes the accent. `TextField` adds a label, help and an error: over and under the field on iOS, a floating label and supporting text on Android.

### Text field

`TextField` is an Input with a `label`, a `description` under it and an `errorMessage` that takes the description's place and marks the field invalid. `required` adds an asterisk to the label. On iOS the label stands over the field (15/20 medium) and the help 4pt under it; on Android the label floats into the outline (or to the top of a filled field) when the field has focus or a value, and the help is Material's supporting text.

Every other prop of React Native's `TextInput` passes through to the text input (`keyboardType`, `secureTextEntry`, `autoCapitalize`, `onFocus`, `onBlur`, `readOnly` and so on). A read-only field shows no clear button.
