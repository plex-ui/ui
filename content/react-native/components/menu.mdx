The parts follow `@plexui/ui`'s Menu: `Menu.Trigger`, `Menu.Content`, `Menu.Item`, `Menu.Separator` and `Menu.Label`. On iOS the pop-up menu of iOS 26: a 250pt glass panel with radius 34, 17pt rows at least 42 tall with the symbol at the leading edge, 8 under its trigger. On Android Material 3 Expressive's menu (Compose's `DropdownMenuGroup`): `surfaceContainerLow` with 16dp corners, as wide as its items and right at its trigger's edge, 44dp items in Label L with 20dp icons; the first and last item between two separators round to the group's corners. The menu opens over its trigger when the screen ends below it, long menus scroll, and a tap outside closes it.

## Examples

### Items and separators

`Menu.Trigger` takes a Button, or any element with `onPress`, and opens the menu. Choosing an item runs its `onSelect` and closes the menu. A `danger` item is red.

<RNPreview demo="menu/Overview" code={overviewCode} minHeight={440} />

### Labels and descriptions

`Menu.Label` heads a group of items, `description` adds a second line under an item's label, and a `disabled` item is dimmed and takes no presses.

<RNPreview demo="menu/LabelsAndDescriptions" code={labelsAndDescriptionsCode} minHeight={440} />

### Aligned to the end, controlled

`align="end"` lines the menu's trailing edge up with its trigger's, as for a More button at the end of a row. `open` and `onOpenChange` control the menu; a menu opened from outside is placed at its trigger too.

<RNPreview demo="menu/Controlled" code={controlledCode} minHeight={440} />
