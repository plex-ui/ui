### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>A bar of task completion between <code>0</code> and <code>max</code> (100 by default); it animates each change of <code>value</code>, so bind it to any source, here the <code>value</code> slider. <code>indeterminate</code> passes <code>value={'{null}'}</code>: a sweep for a task of unknown length. <code>label</code> puts a label and a readout above the bar, the typical upload or onboarding pattern; the bar takes its name from it with <code>aria-labelledby</code> (the label's <code>nativeID</code>), and without one it needs an <code>aria-label</code>. It reads out the value as a rounded percentage, or what <code>getValueLabel</code> returns. <code>size</code> sets the thickness, 4, 6 or 8 points, and <code>color</code> the filled part, such as <code>secondary</code> grey for a second series beside the first.</p>
  <p>On iOS the bar is drawn from the phone layer's tokens: the ink on <code>--progress-track-color</code>, a change of value in 120ms, and a sweep of 40% of the track there and back in 1.5 seconds. On Android it is Material's line progress in the Plex Android look: the accent on the text color at 8% (16% at night), a change of value in 300ms decelerating, and Material's two lines running along the track for a task of unknown length. With reduced motion a value lands at once and the sweep stands still in the middle; a screen reader reads the bar as busy then.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} codeValues={{ value: 66, indeterminate: false, label: true, size: "sm", color: "primary" }}>
  <RNExample demo="progress/Overview" />
</ComponentPreview>

### Segments

<ComponentPreview details={<>Pass <code>segments</code> for parts of one bar side by side, each in its color, such as a bill paid in parts: what is paid, what is pending, and the track left for what is due. A part is <code>{"{ value, color, label }"}</code>; the bar&apos;s value is their sum, and a part past <code>max</code> is cut to it. A hairline of the track (<code>--progress-segment-gap</code>) parts them. It reads out each part with its label and share, such as Paid 60%, Pending 23%.</>} code={segmentsCode}>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Progress view (linear) | Linear progress indicator |
| Look | A 4pt track of the ink on a light gray, rounded; a sweep for an unknown length (iOS has none of its own) | The accent on a light track, rounded; two lines running along it for an unknown length |
