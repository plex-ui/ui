### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p><code>ShimmerText</code> applies a continuous shimmer to text: for loading states, AI-generated content in progress, or visual emphasis. It takes the props of <code>Text</code> for its size, weight and color, as the web's classes. <code>as</code> names the element it stands for: <code>h1</code> to <code>h6</code> are read as a heading, and <code>p</code>, <code>span</code> or <code>div</code> (default) as text.</p>
  <p><code>ShimmerableText</code> turns the shimmer on and off with <code>shimmer</code>, for text that moves between a loading and an idle state. Set to <code>false</code>, the animation finishes its current sweep and then pauses, with no abrupt cut. On iOS the band is the web's: half the text's width, from the text color to a light gray and back, across in 2.1 seconds every 3, the first after 0.6 seconds. On Android it is the Plex Android look's flicker: a 160dp band across the text in 1.2 seconds, linear. With reduced motion the text stands plain.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} codeValues={{ as: "div", shimmer: true }} previewMinHeight={168}>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | None: the Plex shimmer (SwiftUI's shimmering text is a modifier of its own) | None: the Plex shimmer |
| Look | A light band sweeping across the text every 3 seconds | A light band, 160dp wide, crossing the text in 1.2 seconds |
