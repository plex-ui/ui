The props follow `@plexui/ui`'s Avatar. Neither iOS nor Android has an avatar control of its own, so both platforms draw the Plex avatar from the same tokens: the initial at half the size in semibold, a picture with a hairline border inside its edge, and the semantic colors of the soft and solid fills. In a group, each avatar is cut out of the one before it by a 3pt ring of the surface: `--color-surface` on iOS, Material's `surface` on Android.

## Examples

### Overview

Without other props an avatar shows the first letter of `name`. `imageUrl` puts a picture over it; while the picture loads, or when it fails, the initial stays. `Icon` shows a symbol instead of the initial, and `overflowCount` shows `+N`.

<RNPreview demo="avatar/Overview" code={overviewCode} />

### Colors

Six semantic colors, each with a soft and a solid fill.

<RNPreview demo="avatar/Colors" code={colorsCode} />

### Sizes

`size` sets the width and height in points; the initial, symbol and count scale with it. The default is 28.

<RNPreview demo="avatar/Sizes" code={sizesCode} />

### As a button

`onPress` makes the avatar a button, named by `name` for screen readers. It dims while pressed.

<RNPreview demo="avatar/AsButton" code={asButtonCode} />

### Group

`AvatarGroup` overlaps its avatars and gives each one its `size`. Put an avatar with `overflowCount` last for the people the group does not show.

<RNPreview demo="avatar/Group" code={groupCode} />
