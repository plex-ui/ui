`toast()` follows sonner on the web: `toast("Saved")`, `toast.success`, `toast.error`, `toast.warning`, `toast.info` and `toast.dismiss`. Render one `<Toaster />` near the root of the app, inside the `ThemeProvider`. On iOS a toast is a glass banner from the top of the screen (radius 24, a 15pt semibold title, the type's symbol and an action in a 34pt capsule), up to three at once, swiped up to go. On Android it is Material 3's snackbar at the bottom: `inverseSurface` with 4dp corners, Body M, the action in `inversePrimary`, one at a time while the others wait their turn, swiped down to go. A screen reader announces each toast.

### Title and description

A toast stays 4 seconds by default.

### Types

On iOS each type shows its symbol before the title, in its semantic color; `icon` gives a toast a symbol of its own. The snackbar of Android has no symbols.

<RNPreview demo="toast/Types" minHeight={220} code={typesCode} />

### Action and close button

One `action` runs its `onClick` and closes the toast. `duration: Infinity` keeps a toast until it is closed or dismissed, and `closeButton` adds the snackbar's close icon on Android; iOS banners have none, a swipe up closes them. `toast.dismiss()` closes every toast, `toast.dismiss(id)` one; a toast shown again with the same `id` is updated in place.
