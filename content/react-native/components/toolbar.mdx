## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component, top | Navigation bar | Action bar, drawn as glass circles and capsules |
| Native component, bottom | Toolbar | A bottom row: a field with its circles, or one wide action |
| Look | Glass items over the content, 44pt at the top and 48pt at the bottom; a centered 17pt title or a 34pt large title | 46dp circles and capsules of see-through glass (the card at 80%, a 0.55dp rim, a soft shadow in light) 6dp from the edges, a centered Medium 17 title in a 62dp bar, the screen's color fading under them |
| `background="hard"`, top | An opaque bar of the canvas color with a hairline, once content is under it | The classic bar: 56dp in the cards' color with a 3dp shadow, plain icons in 48dp targets, a Medium 18 title from 72dp |

iOS calls only the bottom bar a toolbar; here `Toolbar` is both bars, and `placement` picks one.

### Compound parts

| Part | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `Toolbar.Leading` | Back, Close or menu controls at the leading edge |
| `Toolbar.Title` | Title of the screen, with an optional subtitle |
| `Toolbar.Content` | Middle content: a search field, a main action or a segmented control. `fill` stretches it between the leading and trailing items |
| `Toolbar.Group` | Related buttons in one capsule: of glass on iOS and on Android |
| `Toolbar.Trailing` | Actions at the trailing edge |
| `Toolbar.Row` | A row under the controls, part of the bar: a segmented control or a search field across its width; several stand in their order |

`Toolbar.Leading`, `Toolbar.Trailing`, `Toolbar.Group`, `Toolbar.Content` and `Toolbar.Row` take a View's props.
