The parts follow `@plexui/ui`'s Toolbar: `Toolbar.Leading`, `Toolbar.Title`, `Toolbar.Content`, `Toolbar.Group`, `Toolbar.Trailing` and `Toolbar.Row`. On iOS the items of the bar stand in glass over the content, 44pt at the top and 48 at the bottom, with a centered 17pt title or a 34pt large title under them. On Android the top bar is Material 3's small top app bar (64dp, a Title L title at the start, the navigation icon in `onSurface` and the actions in `onSurfaceVariant`) or its large flexible one, and the bottom bar a docked toolbar in `surfaceContainer`. A neutral Button or IconButton in a bar takes the bar's size, in glass on iOS. By default the bar pads itself for the status bar or the home indicator; the examples turn that off with `safeArea={false}`.

### Navigation buttons

`NavigationButton` is a bar's back, close or confirm button with the kit's glyphs: on iOS a 44pt circle, glass for back and close and the accent for confirm in a bar; on Android a Material icon button, confirm filled. It names itself ("Back", "Close", "Confirm") and leaves the navigation to its `onPress`. `subtitle` adds a second line under the title.

<RNPreview demo="toolbar/Navigation" minHeight={120} code={navigationCode} />

### Grouped actions

`Toolbar.Group` puts related actions in one capsule of glass on iOS; on Android they stand side by side. `titleAlign="start"` puts the title after the leading items, as Android does by default; `titleAlign="center"` gives Android Material's center-aligned app bar.

<RNPreview demo="toolbar/Group" minHeight={120} code={groupCode} />

### Large title

`titleStyle="large"` sets the title on a row of its own under the controls: 34pt bold on iOS, Material's large flexible app bar (36/44) on Android.

<RNPreview demo="toolbar/LargeTitle" minHeight={180} code={largeTitleCode} />

### Content in the bar

`Toolbar.Content` takes the place of the title: a segmented control, a single centered action or, with `fill`, a search field across the row. A segmented control in a bar is the large glass one on iOS.

<RNPreview demo="toolbar/Content" minHeight={120} code={contentCode} />

### A row under the controls

`Toolbar.Row` adds a row under the controls, part of the bar, whose control fills it: a segmented control that switches the screen's views, or a search field. Several rows stand in their order.

<RNPreview demo="toolbar/Row" minHeight={140} code={rowCode} />

### Scrolled content

`scrolled` says content is under the bar, and `background` sets how the bar meets it on iOS: `glass` (the top bar's default) a translucent blurred bar with a line, `hard` an opaque one, `soft` (the bottom bar's default) a veil with no line, `none` nothing. On Android the top app bar takes `surfaceContainer` once scrolled. Scroll the list, and switch the background.

<RNPreview demo="toolbar/Scrolled" minHeight={320} code={scrolledCode} />

### Bottom toolbar

`placement="bottom"` is the bar at the bottom of the screen: 48pt items on iOS, a solid button there the prominent one; Material's docked toolbar on Android.

<RNPreview demo="toolbar/Bottom" minHeight={120} code={bottomCode} />
