### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>A thin divider between content. <code>orientation="horizontal"</code> (default) separates the rows of a list or the parts of a card; <code>"vertical"</code> stands between inline items, such as a meta strip, and stretches to the height of its row, at least a line of text tall. A separator is <code>decorative</code> by default, so screen readers skip it; set <code>decorative={'{false}'}</code> when the divider marks a real grouping, and it is a <code>separator</code> (with its <code>aria-orientation</code> on the web).</p>
  <p>On iOS it is the web's line on a phone: <code>--separator-color</code> (<code>--color-border</code>) at <code>--separator-thickness</code> (1pt); theme them through the <code>ThemeProvider</code>'s <code>brand</code> tokens. On Android it is the divider between the rows of a card in the Plex Android look: one device pixel of the divider gray. A <a href="/react-native/components/list">List</a> and a <a href="/react-native/components/menu">Menu</a> draw their own separators between rows.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} codeValues={{ orientation: "horizontal", decorative: true }} previewMinHeight={200}>
  <RNExample demo="separator/Overview" />
</ComponentPreview>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | None (a Plex view drawn as the web's line) | None (a Plex view drawn as a divider) |
| Look | A 1pt line in the border color | A hairline in the divider gray |
