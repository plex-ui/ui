### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>Field wraps a control with a label, a description and an error message, and links them: the control is named by the label, described by the description or the error (a hint on a phone, <code>aria-describedby</code> on the web) and marked <code>invalid</code> by the error. <code>required</code> adds an asterisk after the label; it is visual only. The <code>layout</code> control puts the label in its four places.</p>
  <p>On a phone a field is a cell of a <code>List</code> on the grouped background, as in an iOS form, with <code>row</code>: the label above its value (<code>orientation="vertical"</code>: on iOS 15/20 in the secondary label, 13 above and below), values in one column (<code>List columns</code>, 40% into the row), the value at the end of the row, or the placeholder alone (<code>hideLabel</code>), its value starting at the row's edge. The row is the field's frame, so the field draws none. The error stands inside the cell, under the value. The words that explain a field go in the List's <code>footer</code>, and a required field says so in its placeholder, as iOS account forms do. A disabled field dims only its value; <code>disabled</code> on the <code>Field</code> dims its label too.</p>
  <p>On Android the same rows are the Plex Android list rows: the label 16sp beside its field, or 13sp in the secondary gray over a bare 17sp value, 20 in. <code>labelAction</code> holds a link that acts on the field, at the end of the label's line; a row has no label line to hold it, so the link stands under the group in the rows' text. On its own, with no <code>row</code>, the label stands over the control as TextField's (iOS 15/20 Medium, the help 4pt under it); on Android an Input or a Textarea floats it in its outlined field and the help is 14sp under it. The web's <code>size</code>, <code>pill</code> and <code>opticallyAlign</code> stay on the desktop: a row sets the field's height, insets and type.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} previewMinHeight={168}>

</ComponentPreview>

### Group

### Action rows

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>A row that is an action is a ghost <code>Button</code> among a List's rows, as an iOS list draws a button. On Android a 50dp row with 16sp words 20 in, pressed in the list selector.</p>
  <p>The Button's <code>color</code> tints the words: <code>info</code> (or <code>primary</code>) for the info color on iOS and the accent on Android, <code>danger</code> for a destructive row. An icon after the words stands at the row's trailing edge at 24pt, such as an arrow for a link that leaves the app. <code>style={'{{ justifyContent: "center" }}'}</code> centres the words, as Settings puts Reset to Default in a group of its own.</p>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Form cell (a text field in a grouped list) | None: the Plex Android list rows and the outlined text field |
| On its own | The label over the control, the help 4pt under it | The label floating in an Input's outlined field, the help 14sp under it |
| As a row | The label at the start and the value at the end; or the label above the bare value | The 16sp label beside the field; or the 13sp label above the bare 17sp value |
