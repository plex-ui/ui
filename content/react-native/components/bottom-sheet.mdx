<UsageBlock>{`import { BottomSheet } from "@plexui/react-native"`}</UsageBlock>

On iOS the sheet of iOS 26: radius 38 on its top corners, a 36x5 grabber and a centered 17pt semibold title. On Android Material 3's modal bottom sheet: 28dp top corners in `surfaceContainerLow`, the 32x4 drag handle, a Title L title at the start, a 32% scrim and 640dp at most. On both a tap on the dim, a drag down or Back closes it, and the grabber is a button that closes it for VoiceOver and TalkBack. A choice of actions is an [ActionSheet](/react-native/components/action-sheet), a decision a [Dialog](/react-native/components/dialog).

### Scrolling content

Content taller than the screen scrolls inside the sheet, while the drag stays on the handle. A sheet without a title takes an `aria-label`.

<RNPreview demo="bottom-sheet/Scrolling" code={scrollingCode} minHeight={560} />

### Not dismissible

With `dismissible={false}` neither a tap on the dim, a drag, Android's back nor Escape closes the sheet: its content does, through `onOpenChange`.
