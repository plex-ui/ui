const [progress, setProgress] = useState(25)
const [maxDuration, setMaxDuration] = useState(15000)
const [done, setDone] = useState(false) // fills the ring and ends the simulation
const [trackColor, setTrackColor] = useState(undefined) // or "var(--alpha-15)"
const [trackActiveColor, setTrackActiveColor] = useState(undefined) // or "var(--gray-1000)"

{indicator === "LoadingIndicator" && <LoadingIndicator size={size} duration={duration} color={color} />}

    maxDuration={maxDuration}
    done={done}
    trackColor={trackColor}
    trackActiveColor={trackActiveColor}
  />
)}
{indicator === "LoadingDots" && <LoadingDots color={color} />}`

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p><code>indicator</code> picks one of the three. <code>LoadingIndicator</code> is a spinner for general loading states, 20 points by default. On iOS it is the iOS 27 spinner of eight rounded spokes, as the touch stylesheet draws it on the web: up to 16 it takes the kit's Small spokes and from 28 the Large ones, and a <code>strokeWidth</code> replaces the spokes' widths, so the example leaves it unset. On Android it is Material's circular progress in the accent: an arc that surges and turns, its stroke an eighth of the size. <code>color</code> takes a text color, such as <code>danger</code> (the primary text by default on iOS, as the web's <code>currentcolor</code>), and <code>duration</code> sets the time of one turn, as the web's <code>--indicator-rotate-duration</code>.</p>
  <p><code>CircularProgress</code> is a progress ring. Without <code>progress</code> it simulates progress over <code>maxDuration</code> milliseconds, and <code>done</code> fills the ring and ends the simulation; this preview sets it every 4 seconds to start over. <code>progress</code>, from 0 to 100, shows a fixed value instead. <code>size</code> and <code>strokeWidth</code> (at most 4) size the ring, and <code>trackColor</code> and <code>trackActiveColor</code> color its track and its progress, a color or a token such as <code>var(--alpha-15)</code>. <code>LoadingDots</code> shows typing progress with staggered pulsing dots in a text color; on Android they also grow and shrink, on the Plex Android look's typing timing.</p>
  <p>The indicators are decorative unless they get an <code>aria-label</code>: then a screen reader reads a busy progress indicator by that name. With reduced motion they stand still.</p>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Activity indicator (UIActivityIndicatorView) for the spinner; none for the ring and the dots | Circular progress indicator for the spinner; none for the ring and the dots |
| Look | Eight rounded spokes, brightening to the leading one, turning a spoke at a time; the Plex ring and dots | An arc in the accent that surges and turns; the Plex ring, and dots that grow and fade in turn |
