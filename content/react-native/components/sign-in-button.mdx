## Logos

The logos are icons of `@plexui/react-native/icons`, made with `createIcon`, for anywhere else they stand: `AppleLogo`, Apple's mark in the icon's color, and `GoogleLogo`, the four-color G, which keeps its colors whatever `color` says. `MicrosoftLogo` is there too. The Tabler brand icons are other drawings: a monochrome G breaks Google's rules.

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Google | The light, dark or neutral button, a capsule, 16 · 12 · 16 | The same looks, a pill, 12 · 10 · 12 |
