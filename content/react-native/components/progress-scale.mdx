## Examples

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>A scale holds a few bands side by side, equally wide, and a marker at the value: the band the value falls in is lit, and its label under it stands out. On iOS it is Apple&apos;s linear gauge as Health draws a range: capsules 6 tall and 3 apart, the value&apos;s band in the accent and the others in the tertiary fill, a white knob 14 across with a slider thumb&apos;s shadow, the labels in Footnote. On Android it is Material&apos;s linear indicator: bands 4 tall and 4 apart, the value&apos;s in primary, a 4 × 16 handle.</p>
  <p>A screen reader reads it as a progress bar whose value is the band and the share, &quot;Both, 50%&quot;; <code>getValueLabel</code> words it otherwise. Right to left the scale runs from the right.</p>
  <p>A brand sets every part as a token round the scale: <code>--scale-band-height</code>, <code>--scale-band-gap</code>, <code>--scale-band-color</code> and <code>--scale-band-color-on</code>, <code>--scale-marker</code> (<code>knob</code>, <code>handle</code> or <code>drop</code>: a round mark flowing into its band through arcs above and under it), <code>--scale-marker-size</code>, <code>--scale-marker-fillet</code>, <code>--scale-marker-color</code>, <code>--scale-label-gap</code>, <code>--scale-label-size</code>, <code>--scale-label-line-height</code>, <code>--scale-label-color</code> and <code>--scale-label-color-on</code>. <code>marker</code> drop here sets them.</p>
  <p>By default a new value moves the marker (iOS 0.35 s, Android 300 ms). <code>animated</code> as an object brings the first value in too: the marker runs from the scale&apos;s start over <code>value</code>, then the band lights over <code>light</code>. With reduced motion, or <code>animated=&#123;false&#125;</code>, a value lands at once.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} codeValues={{ value: 50, marker: "knob", animated: "change", labels: true }} previewMinHeight={240}>
  <RNExample demo="progress-scale/Overview" />
</ComponentPreview>
