## Examples

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>ActionSheet is React Native only: on the web a choice of actions is a <a href="/components/menu">Menu</a>, and a question with its answers a <a href="/components/dialog">Dialog</a>'s alert. On iOS it is the action sheet of iOS 26 on an iPhone: a glass panel of 48pt capsule actions with the title and message over them, drawn as the alert's. With a <code>source</code> the panel stands at the button it opens from, with no cancel capsule (see From its button); without one it stands in the middle of the screen, Cancel the last capsule of the same panel. On Android it is a menu sheet, a <a href="/react-native/components/bottom-sheet">Bottom Sheet</a> with a list of 48dp rows in 16sp, each icon in a 56dp slot at the start (with no icons the labels start 16dp in). It has no cancel row there: a tap outside, a drag down or Back closes it.</p>
  <p>One sheet with every setting: the actions of the web Menu's Destructive items, Rename, Duplicate and Delete project, the destructive one last. An action runs its <code>onPress</code> and closes the sheet. As the web Dialog's alerts and sheets do in its phone preview, the sheet here opens with the phone's screen.</p>

A <code>destructive</code> action is red, its icon too on Android. ## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Action sheet (confirmation dialog) | Modal bottom sheet with a list |
| Look | A glass panel of capsules: in the middle of the screen with Cancel its last capsule, or at its `source` with no Cancel | A menu sheet: 48dp rows, each icon in a 56dp slot, no handle |
