<UsageBlock>{`import { ActionSheet } from "@plexui/react-native"`}</UsageBlock>

ActionSheet is React Native only: the web offers a [Menu](/components/menu). On iOS it is the action sheet of iOS 26 on an iPhone: a glass panel of 48pt capsule actions with the title and message over them, and the cancel capsule apart under it. On Android it is a Material 3 modal [BottomSheet](/react-native/components/bottom-sheet) with a list of 56dp rows, each with its icon. It has no cancel row: a tap outside, a drag down or Back closes it.

## Examples

### Title and message

The button opens the sheet; an action runs its `onPress` and closes it. A `destructive` action is red. iOS leaves the icons out, as UIAlertController does.

<RNPreview demo="action-sheet/Overview" code={overviewCode} minHeight={520} />

### Actions alone

Without a title and a message the sheet holds its actions only. A `disabled` action is dimmed and takes no presses, and `cancelLabel` names the cancel capsule of iOS. Many actions scroll inside the panel, the cancel capsule staying in view.
