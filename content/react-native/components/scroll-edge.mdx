A screen with a bar has its edge in the bar: [Toolbar](/react-native/components/toolbar)'s `background` at the top, [Tab Bar](/react-native/components/tab-bar)'s `fade` at the bottom.

## Examples

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>A screen with no top bar, such as an account page with its own header, lets its content scroll up under the clock and the status icons. <code>ScrollEdge</code> mutes it there, as iOS 27 draws its soft scroll edge (measured on a scroll view's own): from the screen's edge to 34pt past the status bar, the content lightly softened under a wide haze of itself (a 30pt blur, about half of what shows at the edge) and a veil of the screen's color, 49% in light and 59% in dark at the edge, all easing out to nothing 34pt past the status bar. The content does not blur away: it stays faintly there, as on iOS. The top edge shows once <code>scrolled</code> says content is under it; lay it over the screen after the scroll view, in the screen's <code>canvasColor</code> (a grouped screen's gray).</p>
  <p><code>edge="bottom"</code> mutes the content that runs on to the bottom of a screen with no bottom bar, in the same way turned over, from 34pt above the home indicator's safe area; it stays drawn, as iOS draws it, since over the screen's color alone it shows nothing. On Android the top edge is the soft edge of its top bar over the status bar alone, and the bottom one draws nothing.</p>
  <p><code>background="fade"</code> mutes with the screen&apos;s color alone, no blur, as Tab Bar&apos;s <code>fade</code> turned over, the same on iOS and Android: under a floating row of filters over photos, where a blur would turn them to a smudge. Give it the row&apos;s bottom in <code>fadeEnd</code>: the color stands at 85% down to the middle of the row&apos;s 44pt controls (26 above its bottom), then eases out on a smooth curve, about half at the row&apos;s bottom, to nothing 32 below it.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} previewMinHeight={168}>
  <RNExample demo="scroll-edge/Overview" />
</ComponentPreview>

## Platforms

| | iOS 27 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Scroll edge effect, soft | None: the status bar's scrim |
| Look | The content softened under a wide haze and a veil of the screen's color, from the screen's edge to 34pt past the status bar | The screen's color over a light blur under the status bar |
