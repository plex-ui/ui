## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Text styles (Dynamic Type) | Type scale |
| Look | Apple's eleven styles, Large Title 34/41 to Caption 2 11/13; secondary and tertiary text the label at 50% and 25% | Material 3 roles, Headline Large 32/40 to Label Small 11/16, with sizes of its own for some text (Medium 20 for a dialog's or a sheet's title, 13 for a secondary line); secondary and tertiary text in grays, the ink at 50% and 35% |

`variant` keeps Apple's names on both platforms: on Android each one takes the nearest Material role, or a size of its own for `title3` and `footnote`, as the table under Platform text styles lists.
