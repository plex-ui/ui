### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>Reach for <code>InputGroup</code> when a field needs a text prefix or suffix, a unit, a menu or a button beside its value.</p>
  <p>On a phone an input group is the value of a <code>List</code> row (<code>Field row</code>): the row's label names it, its prefix, unit, menu or button sits in the row, and the row is its frame. With a prefix the List takes <code>columns</code>, so the value starts at the column, the prefix right before it in the secondary label; without one the value ends at the row's edge with its unit after it, as iOS writes "5 km". A row keeps a prefix or a unit, not both. A header becomes the row's label, a counter or a status line the List's <code>footer</code>, and a main action the full-width button under the List. On Android the row's type is 16sp and the prefix stands 4 from the value.</p>
  <p>Put an <code>InputGroup.Input</code> and <code>InputGroup.Addon</code>s inside an <code>InputGroup</code>. On its own the group is an Input's frame of its <code>variant</code>, <code>size</code> and <code>pill</code>, with the inline addons as the field's adornments, and it passes <code>disabled</code> and <code>invalid</code> down to the control and the buttons. <code>align</code> places an addon before (<code>inline-start</code>) or after (<code>inline-end</code>) the control, or as a row above (<code>block-start</code>) or below (<code>block-end</code>) it inside the frame. <code>loading</code> shows a <code>LoadingIndicator</code> in an addon while a value is checked. A row sets the field's height, so the example shows no <code>size</code>, <code>variant</code>, <code>pill</code> or <code>align</code> control.</p>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | None: a form cell's text field with its prefix or unit | None: a Plex Android row's field with its prefix or unit |
| In a List row | The row is the frame; a prefix right before the value, a unit right after it, in the secondary label | The row is the frame; 16sp prefix and unit 4 from the value |
| On its own | An Input's frame with the addons inside it | The outlined field with the addons inside it |

### InputGroup.Input

The group's text field, without a frame of its own: every `Input` prop except `variant`, `size`, `pill` and the adornments, which the group sets. `disabled` and `invalid` default to the group's state.

### InputGroup.Textarea

The same with several lines: `rows` (3 by default), `autoResize` and `maxRows`, as `Textarea`.

### InputGroup.Addon

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "align", type: '"inline-start" | "inline-end" | "block-start" | "block-end"', default: '"inline-start"', description: "Before or after the control on its line, or a row above or below it" },
  { prop: "style", type: "StyleProp<ViewStyle>", description: "Style of the addon" },
]} />

### InputGroup.Button

Takes the [`Button`](/react-native/components/button) props except `size`, which follows the group. `color` defaults to `"secondary"`, `variant` to `"ghost"`, and `disabled` to the group's value. A ghost `uniform` button is the field's adornment button.

### InputGroup.Text

A `Text` for a prefix, a unit or a counter inside an addon, in the secondary label. Every `Text` prop passes through.
