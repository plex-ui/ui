<UsageBlock>{`import { Slider } from "@plexui/react-native"`}</UsageBlock>

The props follow `@plexui/ui`'s Slider. On iOS the slider is the phone layer's: a 6pt track filled in the range color, a 38x24 white capsule thumb with a soft shadow, in a 52pt row, with the label and the value at 17/22 over it. On Android it is Material 3's slider: a 16dp track, the active part in the accent and the rest in `secondaryContainer`, a 4x44dp handle that narrows while held, 6dp from either part, and a stop at each free end. A press anywhere on the track moves the nearest thumb there, and each thumb is an adjustable element for VoiceOver and TalkBack.

## Examples

### Overview

`label` puts a label over the track, with the value at its end; `unit` follows the value (or `prefixUnit` comes before it).

<RNPreview demo="slider/Overview" code={overviewCode} />

### Adornments and reset

`startAdornment` and `endAdornment` stand at either end of the track. `resetValue` adds a small round reset button before the value while the value is another; a press sets it back.

<RNPreview demo="slider/Adornments" code={adornmentsCode} />

### Steps

`step` snaps the value; the value shows as many decimals as the step has.

<RNPreview demo="slider/Steps" code={stepsCode} />

### Range

`range` gives the slider a thumb for each value of an array. A thumb never passes its neighbor, and `minStepsBetweenThumbs` keeps them apart. On Android this is Material's range slider.

<RNPreview demo="slider/Range" code={rangeCode} />

### Marks

`marks` puts labels under the track, each under the thumb's place at its value. Marks that would crowd each other all hide. `hideValue` hides the value beside the label.

<RNPreview demo="slider/Marks" code={marksCode} />

### Vertical

`orientation="vertical"` stands the slider upright, 200pt tall with its label over the track, the minimum at the bottom. iOS has no vertical slider, so the same track and capsule stand upright; Android draws Material's vertical slider. Marks and adornments are for horizontal sliders only.

<RNPreview demo="slider/Vertical" code={verticalCode} />
