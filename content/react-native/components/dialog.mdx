<UsageBlock>{`import { Dialog } from "@plexui/react-native"`}</UsageBlock>

Dialog is one call with props: the web Dialog's `mobileVariant="alert"`. A sheet from the bottom edge is a [BottomSheet](/react-native/components/bottom-sheet). On iOS it is the alert of iOS 26: a 320pt glass panel with radius 34, the title and message over 48pt capsule actions, the preferred action semibold on the primary fill and a destructive one in red. Two actions stand side by side with the cancel first, three or more stack with the cancel last, and a tap outside does nothing. On Android it is the dialog of Android 16's Settings: Material 3's basic dialog (radius 28, `surfaceContainerHigh`, a Headline S title) whose positive action is a filled button, a cancel an outlined one and any other a text button at the start of the bar. A tap outside or Back closes it there.

## Examples

### Two actions

The button opens the dialog in place of the page; any action closes it and runs its `onPress`. On Android the action that is not a cancel is the filled button, in the error color when it is destructive.

<RNPreview demo="dialog/Overview" code={overviewCode} minHeight={520} />

### Three actions

Three actions stack on iOS, the cancel last. On Android the `preferred` action is the filled button, the cancel outlined and the other a text button at the start. `mobileLayout="stacked"` stacks two actions on iOS too, `"inline"` keeps three in a row.

<RNPreview demo="dialog/ThreeActions" code={threeActionsCode} minHeight={520} />

### With a field

Children stand between the text and the actions, as a text field. The dialog rises over the keyboard, and long content scrolls inside it. A `disabled` action takes no presses until the field has a name.

<RNPreview demo="dialog/WithField" code={withFieldCode} minHeight={520} />
