const [value, setValue] = useState("Gate code 4512\\nThird floor, door on the left\\nCall when you arrive")

    <Textarea
      variant="ghost"
      value={value}
      onChangeText={setValue}
      placeholder="Add a note"
      rows={3}
      maxRows={10}
      autoResize
      autoSelect={autoSelect}
      disabled={disabled}
      invalid={invalid}
    />
  </Field>
</List>`

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p>On a phone, put a textarea under the label of a <code>List</code> row (<code>Field row orientation="vertical"</code>) with <code>variant="ghost"</code>: the row is its frame and sets its insets and type, so the example shows no <code>size</code> or <code>variant</code> control; <code>rows</code> sets its height. On iOS its lines are the row's 17/22 from the row's text start; on Android the bare 17sp lines of a card's text field.</p>
  <p>Textarea is the Input's field with several lines, so it takes the same <code>variant</code>, <code>size</code>, <code>disabled</code>, <code>readOnly</code> and <code>invalid</code>, and on its own the same frames: on iOS a 44pt field's border with 11 above and below its lines, on Android the outlined field, its label floating on the line inside a <code>Field</code>. <code>rows</code> sets the initial height; with <code>autoResize</code> the textarea grows and shrinks as the user types, up to <code>maxRows</code> (the larger of <code>rows</code> and 10 when unset), then scrolls. <code>autoSelect</code> selects all its text when it takes focus (TextInput's <code>selectTextOnFocus</code>). <code>disabled</code> dims it and takes no input. <code>invalid</code> marks it invalid; in a row the error is the row's <code>errorMessage</code> line under the value. Browser autofill extensions are a desktop matter: iOS and Android offer autofill from the system.</p>
</>} code={overviewCode} previewMinHeight={168}>

</ComponentPreview>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Text view (multi-line text field) | Text field, multi-line (outlined) |
| In a List row | The row's bare value under its label, 17/22 lines from the text start | The bare 17sp value under the row's label, 20 in |
| On its own | A framed field, its lines 11 from the border | The outlined field, 8dp corners, the label floating on the line in a Field |
