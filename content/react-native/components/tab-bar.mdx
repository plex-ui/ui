The props follow `@plexui/ui`'s TabBar. On iOS the tab bar of iOS 26: two to five tabs, each a 28pt symbol over a 10pt label, in a floating glass capsule, the selected one on a pill, with an optional search or prominent tab in a glass circle of its own. On Android Material 3 Expressive's navigation bar: 64dp in `surfaceContainer`, each destination a 24dp icon in a 56x32 indicator (`secondaryContainer` when selected) over a Label M label. By default the bar pads itself for the home indicator; the examples turn that off with `safeArea={false}`. The arrow keys move between tabs on the web.

## Examples

### Tabs

`TabBar.Item` is one destination: a `value`, a `label` and an `icon`. Control the selected tab with `value` and `onValueChange`, or let the bar keep it from `defaultValue` (the first tab when there is none).

<RNPreview demo="tab-bar/Overview" minHeight={150} code={overviewCode} />

### Search tab

`search` adds a search tab in a glass circle of its own at the trailing end, as iOS 26's separate search tab. On Android it is a plain destination of the bar.

<RNPreview demo="tab-bar/Search" minHeight={120} code={searchCode} />

### Prominent tab

`prominent` lifts one destination out of the capsule into the circle, its symbol alone with `label` as its accessible name. With `search` too, the prominent tab takes the circle and search becomes the last tab of the capsule. On Android both are plain destinations.

<RNPreview demo="tab-bar/Prominent" minHeight={120} code={prominentCode} />

### Badges

`badge` shows a count or a short word in a red capsule over the symbol, read after the label ("Chats, 128"). A count of 0 shows none, as on iOS; `true` draws Material's small dot on Android and an empty capsule on iOS.

<RNPreview demo="tab-bar/Badges" minHeight={120} code={badgesCode} />

### Disabled tab

<RNPreview demo="tab-bar/Disabled" minHeight={120} code={disabledCode} />

### Accessory

`accessory` puts content in a glass capsule above the tabs, as iOS 26's bottom accessory (a song playing, a draft). Material has none: on Android it is a 48dp row above the bar.

<RNPreview demo="tab-bar/Accessory" minHeight={170} code={accessoryCode} />

### Minimized on scroll

`minimized` shows the selected tab alone in a 48pt circle, as iOS 26's `tabBarMinimizeBehavior`; a tap on it expands the bar. `useTabBarMinimize` drives it from a scroll: the bar minimizes while the content scrolls down and expands when it scrolls back up or reaches the top. Scroll the list on iOS. Android's navigation bar does not minimize.

### useTabBarMinimize

`useTabBarMinimize()` returns `{ minimized, onMinimizedChange, onScroll, scrollEventThrottle }`: pass `onScroll` and `scrollEventThrottle` to the ScrollView, `minimized` and `onMinimizedChange` to the TabBar. A scroll of 8 points in one direction flips the bar.
