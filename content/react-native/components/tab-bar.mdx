## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Tab bar | A glass capsule of tabs, in place of Material's navigation bar |
| Look | A glass capsule floating over the content, the selected tab on a pill | A 56dp capsule of see-through glass (the card at 76%, a 0.4dp rim, a soft shadow) 12dp from the edges and 8dp above the gesture bar, at least 320dp of tabs: each a 24dp symbol over a 12sp label, the selected one in the accent on its own 9% pill; search or a prominent tab in a 56dp circle beside it (ours) |

Android's Tabs are a different component (they switch views inside one screen): use [SegmentedControl](/react-native/components/segmented-control) for those.

### useTabBarMinimize

`useTabBarMinimize()` returns `{ minimized, onMinimizedChange, onScroll, scrollEventThrottle }`: pass `onScroll` and `scrollEventThrottle` to the ScrollView, `minimized` and `onMinimizedChange` to the TabBar. A scroll of 8 points in one direction flips the bar, and the top of the content expands it.

The bar is a tab list named Tabs, each tab a tab with its selected state, and a badge is read after the label. Minimized, the selected tab that stays expands the bar when pressed.
