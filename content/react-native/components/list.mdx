export const countCode = `import { Checkbox, List, ListItem } from "@plexui/react-native"

const STEPS = ["Add a profile photo", "Verify your email", "Invite your team"]
const [done, setDone] = useState([true, true, false])

<List header="Set up your account" action={\`\${done.filter(Boolean).length}/\${STEPS.length}\`}>
  {STEPS.map((step, index) => (
    <ListItem
      key={step}
      label={step}
      trailing={
        <Checkbox
          checked={done[index]}
          onCheckedChange={(on) => setDone((list) => list.map((value, i) => (i === index ? on : value)))}
          accessibilityLabel={step}
        />
      }
    />
  ))}
</List>`

### Overview

<ComponentPreview details={<>
  <p><code>List</code> is a section of rows and <code>ListItem</code> one row, as <code>@plexui/ui</code>&apos;s FieldGroup and NavigationRow on a phone: use it for settings and for rows that open a page, and keep a section to related rows under one header.</p>
  <p>One Settings screen with every setting of a list, as the React <a href="/components/navigation-row">Navigation Row</a> Overview sets its rows in a FieldGroup: Time zone, Start week on, Day starts at and Default alerts show their current <code>value</code> at the end, and a long one (<code>valuePlacement="auto"</code>, Default alerts) moves under the label when it does not fit beside it, and a press opens the page of their choices, where the current one carries the checkmark and a press picks another and goes back. A row with <code>onPress</code> is one button, and shows the chevron on iOS; <code>disclosure</code> drops it, for rows that open a record rather than a page of choices. <code>description</code> adds a line under the label, and the row grows to hold it; a pressable row reads it after its name. As in iOS Settings, a description goes with a control at the end (Week numbers and its switch), never with a value: what a row with a value means goes in the section&apos;s <code>footer</code>. A <code>disabled</code> row greys all of it and can&apos;t be pressed. <code>trailing</code> ends a row with a control, here a Switch: in a row without <code>onPress</code> a Switch or a Checkbox there toggles on a press anywhere on the row, as the web&apos;s phone rows, an option of a RadioGroup around the list is chosen by it, and on Android a Select there opens its choice from it; the control stays the one element for screen readers, named by the label. A <code>destructive</code> row, Sign out in a section of its own, is red and shows no chevron.</p>
  <p><code>header</code> titles a section and names it for screen readers, <code>description</code> adds a line under the title, <code>footer</code> a note under the rows and <code>error</code> a message in the danger color in its place (a row has its own <code>error</code>, under the row, as a form row's); on Android the header is the first row of the card, 14sp Medium in the accent (a lighter blue in dark mode), and the footer a 14sp gray note under the card, 10dp above it and 17dp below. <code>leading</code> puts a symbol at the start: with <code>leadingColor</code> iOS draws it white on a rounded tile of that color, the look of the Settings app, and Android a white 24dp symbol on a 28dp tile of that color with 10dp corners, with no divider under the row; without it Android draws the symbol plain in the text color (a gray in dark mode). <code>surface</code> is the canvas the section stands on: <code>grouped</code>, the gray grouped background of a settings screen with white sections, or <code>plain</code>, a plain screen with sections in the plain secondary gray, the same on both platforms. A List sets no space around itself: the screen spaces its sections, 35pt apart on iOS less what a header or a footer between them takes, as the React FieldGroup does, and 12dp from the screen's edges and 12dp apart on Android.</p>

## Platforms

| | iOS 26 | Android |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native component | Inset grouped list | List (a Settings preference group) |
| Look | A section with 26pt corners, 52pt rows 20pt in from its edges, hairlines from the text's start, the value at the end and a chevron on a row that opens a page | One card of the section's rows with 16dp corners: rows 50dp tall at least, the label 16sp, the value 16sp in the accent 17dp from the end, a divider from the text's start (none under a row with a tile); no chevron |
| `surface="plain"` | The section in the secondary gray on a white screen | A plain list: no card, the rows across the screen, the header a 32dp gray band |
