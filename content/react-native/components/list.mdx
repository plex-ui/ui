<UsageBlock>{`import { List, ListItem } from "@plexui/react-native"`}</UsageBlock>

`List` is a section of rows and `ListItem` one row, as `@plexui/ui`'s FieldGroup and NavigationRow on a phone. On iOS an inset grouped section of iOS 26: 26pt corners, 52pt rows with their content 20pt in from the section's edges, hairlines between rows from the text's start, a chevron on a row that opens a page. On Android a preference group of Android 16's Settings: every row its own card in `surfaceBright` on the `surfaceContainer` page, 2dp apart, 20dp at the group's outer corners and 4dp where rows meet, rows 72dp tall with the title in Title M; a row's `value` shows as its summary line under the title, and a drill-in has no chevron.

## Examples

### Overview

A row with `onPress` opens something: on iOS it shows a chevron. `value` is the current setting, `trailing` a control at the end.

<RNPreview demo="list/Overview" grouped code={overviewCode} />

### Header and footer

`header` titles the section and names it for screen readers; `footer` is a note under it. On Android the header is the Settings category, in the primary color.

<RNPreview demo="list/HeaderFooter" grouped code={headerFooterCode} />

### Leading symbols

`leading` puts a symbol at the start. With `leadingColor`, iOS draws it white on a rounded tile of that color, the look of the Settings app; Android draws the symbol plain, in `onSurfaceVariant`.

<RNPreview demo="list/Leading" grouped code={leadingCode} />

### Descriptions and controls

`description` adds secondary text under the label; a pressable row reads it to screen readers after its name. Any control can end a row: a Switch, a Badge.

<RNPreview demo="list/Descriptions" grouped code={descriptionsCode} />

### Disabled and destructive

A `disabled` row dims and takes no presses. A `destructive` row is red, for an action such as signing out; give it a section of its own, and `disclosure={false}` since it opens no page.

<RNPreview demo="list/States" grouped code={statesCode} />

### Plain surface

`surface="plain"` is for a list on a plain screen rather than the grouped background of a settings screen: the section takes the plain secondary gray on iOS, and its rows `surfaceContainer` on a `surface` page on Android.

<RNPreview demo="list/Plain" code={plainCode} />
