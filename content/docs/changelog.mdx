## Unreleased

### Added

- `ColorPicker` and `ColorWell` in React and Vue, after the iOS 26 kit Color Pickers. The picker chooses a color from Grid (the kit's 120 colors, the chosen one in a white ring), Spectrum (the hues from top to bottom, white through the full hue to black across, as the system picker draws it) or Sliders (red, green and blue, and a hex field), sets its Opacity (`supportsAlpha`), and keeps saved colors (`swatches`) in pages of ten with Add after the last; Delete or Backspace, or Delete in a saved color's context menu, removes one. The well is the kit's Picker Button, 28px or 36px, the color inside a rainbow ring or the rainbow alone with no color; it opens the picker in a Popover, and on a phone in a floating glass sheet of the kit's size with the eyedropper (where the browser has the EyeDropper API), the title and Close. Colors are `#rrggbb`, or `#rrggbbaa` with opacity. New icon: `Eyedropper`.
- `ContextMenu` in React and Vue: the actions of an element, after the iOS 26 kit Context Menu. A secondary click opens the menu at the pointer; a touch and hold of half a second opens it 18px below the element, at the edge on the side of the touch, over the kit's dimming (black at 23% over a 30px blur) with the element cut out in its own corners, or the kit's 30px, and the kit's shadow around it; the press that opened it neither activates the element nor moves focus off the menu. In a Dialog or a Sheet the dimming covers it too, and the menu stays open when the finger lifts. Shift F10 or the Context Menu key opens it under a focused element and closing returns focus there. The items are the Menu ones, also under `ContextMenu`. With several context menus on a page, each one's anchor, dimming and menu reach the body together and in that order, in Vue as in React.
- `TabBar` in React and Vue: the tab bar of a phone screen after the iOS 26 kit Tab Bar - iPhone: two to five sections, each a PlexUI icon over a 10px label, in a floating capsule of the shared mobile glass 21px from the sides and the bottom, the selected tab on an `alpha-08` pill in the primary text and the others in the Sidebar item gray. `search` adds a search tab in a 62px glass circle of its own at the trailing end. `minimizeBehavior="onScrollDown"` shrinks the bar to 48px circles of the selected tab and search while the `Page` content scrolls down, and brings it back on a scroll up or a tap on the tab that stays, as `tabBarMinimizeBehavior` does. The bar reserves its height on the `Page`, like a bottom `Toolbar`, and never renders from 768px up. The tabs are buttons, or links with `href`, and the selected one has `aria-current="page"`.
- `PageControl` in React and Vue: one dot for each page, the current one solid, after the iOS 26 kit Page control: 8px dots 8px apart in a 24px capsule, the other pages at 30%, a 44px target on a phone. From seven pages the dots past a window around the current page shrink to 6px and 4px and the rest hide, as the kit variants show. A tap on the leading or trailing side of the current dot moves one page, a mouse or pen picks a dot, a drag scrubs page by page and past either end reaches the first or last page (`onChange` tells `scrubbing`), and the keyboard moves as on a slider. `background` shows the capsule fill while interacting (`automatic`), always (`prominent`) or never (`minimal`, which does not scrub), as UIPageControl background styles do.
- `Stepper` in React and Vue: two segments that decrease and increase a number between `min` and `max` by `step`, with optional `wrap`, hold to repeat (`autoRepeat`), a `name` that submits the value, and the keyboard of a spin button (Up, Down, Page Up, Page Down, Home, End). It shows no value of its own; the value sits beside it, as in iOS 26. A segment at an end of the range is dimmed. On a phone it takes the iOS 26 kit size, a 92 x 32 capsule with 46px segments, a 44px touch target and 22px icons, on a PlexUI alpha fill; on a desktop it uses the PlexUI control size and the Segmented Control track color.
- Dialog docs: a Text field example, an alert that asks for one short value with a 48px capsule field, its text selected on open, in React and Vue.
- Popover docs: a Confirmation example in React and Vue, a destructive action confirmed from the button that asks for it. On a phone the panel covers its button, as iOS 26 presents an action sheet from its source: no Cancel button, since a tap anywhere else cancels, the message at 17/22 in the primary label 31px from the edge and the action as a 48px capsule 16px from the edges, as an iPhone confirmation popover measures. Opening focuses the panel, not the action. The shared styles are `components/docs/PopoverConfirmation.module.css`.
- One `background` for every bar of a phone screen, after the scroll edge styles of iOS 26 (kit Materials, Scroll Edge Effect - Soft and - Hard): `soft`, the canvas color over the content under the controls fading out past them; `hard`, an opaque bar of the canvas color with a 1px line (`--page-bar-hard-line`, black 12% in light, as the kit's Hard edge); `glass`, a translucent, blurred bar with a hairline; and `solid`, an opaque bar with no line. `Toolbar` (top and bottom), `Page.Header`, `Page.Footer`, the bars of a `Dialog` Sheet and `TabBar` take it in React and Vue, with a `background` control in their docs. Hard and glass show themselves only while content is under the bar: under a top bar once the content has scrolled, over a bottom bar while it continues below, which bottom bars mark with `data-content-below`. `Dialog.Body` now reports both to the Sheet's header, footer and toolbars, and `Page.Content` reports content below to the footer, the bottom toolbar and the tab bar. The type is `BarBackground`, from the Toolbar entry.

### Changed

- `Page.Header`, `Page.Footer` and the bars of a `Dialog` Sheet default to `background="soft"`, the soft scroll edge of iOS 26 that a phone `Toolbar` already had, instead of `"glass"`; the translucent bar stays available as `background="glass"`, and on a footer it now shows only while content is under it. `Toolbar`'s `background="edge"` is now called `"soft"`; `"edge"` still works and renders as soft. A large title on a page keeps a hard or glass bar without its line until the inline title comes in.
- `Field` rows: a text prefix of the value, an `Input` start adornment or an `InputGroup.Text` addon at the start, is followed by the value with no gap (`https://acme.com`, `$0.00`), as iOS writes it. An icon, a button and a trailing unit keep their gap. This applies to ghost rows everywhere and to every row on a phone.
- `Tabs` (and `SegmentedControl`) on a phone are the iOS 26 glass segmented control (kit Toolbars 5815:55167) in React and Vue. Horizontal tabs of either variant are one 44px capsule of the shared mobile glass, as toolbar buttons are, with segments of one width 8px from its ends and a 36px selection reaching 4px past its segment (`--segmented-control-thumb-outset`); a segment never narrows below its label, so tabs that do not fit still scroll, and `block` fills the row and shortens the labels. Underline tabs take that capsule, since iOS has no underline tabs. Vertical tabs of either variant are an inset grouped list (kit Lists Row): 52px rows with the 17px label, a 22px icon in a 28px column, separators from the text and a checkmark on the selected row instead of the thumb, which the new `TabCheckmark` element shows only there.
- Docs: on a phone the Tabs examples stand on the grouped screen 16px from its edges, where horizontal tabs fill the row as the kit examples do and vertical tabs are a list card. The `variant`, `flush` and `block` controls are desktop-only, and the underline sections and Vertical underline have no Phone preview, since they repeat the segmented ones (React and Vue).
- `Checkbox` (solid and pill) marks a choice with the PlexUI `Check` icon, and `Minus` when indeterminate, in React and Vue instead of drawn arms, the same icons as the ghost variant. The icon fills the indicator (`--checkbox-check-icon-size`, the indicator size by default) and keeps `--checkbox-checkmark-color`; a new mark is revealed from the left and a cleared one fades out.
- Phone layout margin: Page and Toolbar gutters, Sheet bodies and header actions, row insets, separators, and group titles and footers share `--mobile-layout-margin`, 16px up to 413px wide and 20px from 414px, as native iOS 26 screens at 430pt put bar buttons, groups and row text at 20. The 402px phone preview keeps 16, and the Settings, sign-in and New Album examples use the same margin as every other form instead of their own 20.
- Page, Toolbar and Sheet titles are centered on their 44px bar, so the title sits on the buttons' center as in native iOS 26 bars, 2px higher than before.
- `SidebarMobileMenuButton` (React and Vue) shows the PlexUI `SidebarMenuMobile` icon, which turns into `X` while the menu is open, instead of drawn lines. Its tokens are `--sidebar-mobile-icon-size` (now `24px`, the icon canvas), `--sidebar-mobile-icon-color` and `--sidebar-mobile-icon-transition-duration`; `--sidebar-mobile-line-*` are removed.
- `Dialog` on a phone: a Sheet or an Alert dims the page with the new `--dialog-mobile-scrim`, black at 20% in light as native iOS 26 does (a white page shows at 204), instead of 50%. The dark theme keeps 50%, as a native dark Alert does, and desktop keeps its 50% dim.
- Phone surfaces and glass use PlexUI colors. The iOS background roles (`--mobile-surface-*`, plain, grouped and elevated) take the nearest PlexUI gray of each theme instead of iOS hex values: `#f2f2f7` becomes `--gray-75`, `#1c1c1e` `--gray-150`, `#2c2c2e` `--gray-300`, `#3a3a3c` `--gray-350`, and a black dark canvas `--gray-0`. The glass (`--mobile-glass-background` and its hover and press) is `--gray-50` at 64% in light and, after native iOS 26 dark screens, `--alpha-12` in dark (a black page shows at 31, a dark Sheet at 57, as natively) instead of a 19/19/19 fill at 42% that read darker than the page; hover and press are `--alpha-16` and `--alpha-20`. Bar buttons, Toolbar groups, the toolbar search field and the Segmented composer track change with it; the composer thumb is `--gray-200` in dark, darker than its track as natively.
- `SegmentedControl` and segmented `Tabs` on a phone take the glass of toolbar buttons: the track is `--mobile-glass-background` with its sheen, rim and blur (the new `--segmented-control-sheen`, `--segmented-control-shadow` and `--segmented-control-backdrop-filter`, which `touch.css` sets), and the thumb `--segmented-control-mobile-thumb-background`, darker than the glass as natively (`--alpha-08` in light, `--gray-200` in dark), with no shadow. Every label is 15px Medium, the selected one in the primary label color and the others secondary, as the kit and Safari's tab groups draw them; labels take no tracking of their own, since the system font tracks them. Tabs that do not fit scroll inside the capsule with segments as wide as their labels, as in Safari and Telegram. The Segmented composer header keeps its 48px control on the same glass with both labels primary, as Calendar draws it, and the color picker's Mode control stays the kit picker's plain 36px segmented control.
- `Sidebar` drawer on a phone (React and Vue) is an iOS 26 grouped list, as the sidebar of iPhone Mail and Freeform is: the drawer takes the grouped canvas (`--sidebar-mobile-background`), each menu is a card (`--sidebar-mobile-list-radius`, `26px`) of 52px rows with separators from the label, 24px icons in a 40px column, 17px labels and secondary counts. A group label heads its section in semibold 20px. A row that leads to a page ends with the PlexUI `Disclosure` chevron in the tertiary label color; an item with a submenu keeps its own chevron, which turns down as its items open, indented under it. A selected row fills edge to edge and hides the separators at its edges. The tokens are `--sidebar-mobile-list-*` (inset, icon column and size, indent, row height, section gaps, header size and weight, chevron size and offsets). The App sidebar keeps its side drawer.
- `Toolbar` large title: it starts 21px under the row of controls (`--toolbar-large-title-gap`, was 10px), as in iOS 26 Mail and Freeform. On a page it scrolls up with the content under the controls, which then show the title inline, as UIKit does in iOS 26; before, it stayed in place and the list scrolled under it. `Page.Content` writes its scroll offset to the page as `--page-scroll-y`.
- Input docs: on a phone (Phone previews and a narrow window) every example is a row of a `FieldGroup`, as in the Form Examples, in React and Vue. The size, gutter, pill and variant controls stay on the desktop view, since a row sets the input's height, insets and type and `touch.css` makes `sm` to `xl` 44px; behaviour controls such as `disabled` and `invalid` stay. Invalid and validation examples put the message under the row, search points to the Toolbar search field, and the code tab shows the desktop and the phone composition.

### Fixed

- A value or placeholder longer than its room in a `Field` row on a phone (`Input`, `InputGroup`, React and Vue) ends in an ellipsis while it is not being edited, as a text field of an iOS form does, instead of being cut off mid-letter.
- `DateRangePicker` month stepper in a `Field` row on a phone (React and Vue) keeps 7.75em for its month instead of the desktop 9em, the width of September 2026 at the row's 17px, so the row's label stays on one line and the arrows stay put when the month changes. The month gives the stepper its baseline, so it sits on the row's line with the label instead of 1.5px above it.
- Stepper docs (Vue): the In rows example shows its footer note, as the React example does.
- `Input.AdornmentButton` in a `Field` row on a phone (React and Vue) is the row's accessory, as the password eye of the Form Examples sign-in: a 19px glyph centred on its 44px target with its ink at the row's trailing inset, and no pressed fill. The row sets the input to its 22px line, which left the button a 14px face and an 11px glyph.
- `DatePicker` and `DateRangePicker` on a phone mark today again, with the dot they show from 768px up (4px, centered under the date). The phone calendar hid the dot for a colored date that it no longer draws, so today had no mark.
- `Menu` (Vue) closes when the window loses focus, to another app or another frame of the page, as the React menu does (Radix's Menu root). A context menu in a phone preview stayed open after a tap on the page around the preview.
- `Toolbar` top bar on a phone: content scrolled under the row of controls no longer shows through under the title. The soft edge covers the row with the canvas and fades out over the bar's bottom padding and 16px past it, as native iOS 26 bars hide content under the row almost entirely; before, the fade ran from the top of the bar, so text under the title kept about half its contrast.
- `Toolbar` disabled buttons on a phone follow iOS 26: a disabled glass button kept its 44px target but lost its glass and painted a 5% gray circle, also inside `Toolbar.Group`, and a disabled prominent action (`NavigationButton action="confirm"`, a solid Button) showed the shared disabled fill. Now a glass button keeps its glass, or the Group's, and draws its symbol or label in `--mobile-label-tertiary`; a prominent action turns into a gray capsule from the new `--toolbar-prominent-disabled-background` and `--toolbar-prominent-disabled-color` (`--alpha-12` under `--gray-0` in light, `--alpha-20` under `--alpha-30` in dark, the PlexUI colors nearest to native screens). The Toolbar page has a Disabled example in React and Vue.
- `Dialog.Content` and `Menu.Content` (React) call the owner's latest `onCloseAutoFocus` when the close and an owner state change render together. The closing content is frozen for its exit, so the handler from before the close ran and focus went to the wrong place. Vue already read the current handler.
- Rows with actions example: after Delete account, focus moves to Restore sample account; after restoring, it moves to the first action of the restored account instead of falling to the page.
- Settings with selection pages example: on a phone a disabled row (Weekly digest) draws its label, value and disclosure in the iOS tertiary label color, as a disabled Menu item does, so it no longer reads like an enabled row's secondary value.
- Form examples on a phone (Settings with selection pages, Tags in rows, Rows with actions): the disclosure chevron of a row is the tertiary label, lighter than the row value, as in iOS 26 Settings and Mail (a value at 138 and its chevron at 197 on white), instead of the secondary color. A disabled row's disclosure keeps the tertiary label too.
- Floating view options example (Dialog): the page under the sheet showed only the top edge of its Current view group on both Desktop and Phone, because the preview stage took the height set on it. The screen now carries its height, as in the Settings example, and its title fits on a phone.
- The password eye in sign-in and account rows is drawn on a 19px canvas, within 1px of the iOS row accessory, instead of 20px.

## 0.29.0 (2026-11-06)

### Added

- `Toolbar` for React and Vue: a phone-only top or bottom bar after the iOS 26 Toolbar Top, Top Sheet and Bottom with `Toolbar.Leading`, `Toolbar.Title` (subtitle, large and compact titles), `Toolbar.Content` (`fill` for a search field or one wide action), `Toolbar.Group` capsules and `Toolbar.Trailing`. - Focus returns to the trigger after the exit, and only if it was not moved somewhere else in the meantime. `Select` still gives focus back as it closes, so a key pressed right away reaches the field.
- A closing overlay is `inert`: out of the tab order and of hit testing, so the page takes clicks during the exit. `TransitionGroup` has a new `overlay` prop for this.
- The Vue `Dialog`, `Menu` and `Popover` keep their slots live during the exit. State you clear together with the close can blank that part of the panel for the length of the exit; it no longer throws.
- `--color-text-danger-soft` in the dark theme moves from red 400 to red 200, so soft danger text reads at 4.8:1 on its tint over an elevated panel, up from 3.18:1. Soft `Button`, `Badge`, `Avatar` and `StatCard` trends pick this up.

### Fixed

- `Popover.Content` passes `data-*` attributes on to the panel. It dropped them.
- `InputGroup` no longer sets `role="group"` on every instance: a group without a name adds nothing for assistive technology. Pass `role` yourself when you want it.
- `Select` opens on Enter, as a combobox button does. It already opened on Space and the arrow keys.
- A `Field` label opens the `Select` it belongs to. Its `for` reached the hidden input and only moved focus to the trigger.
- `Field` keeps an `id` its child already has and points the label at it, instead of replacing it silently.
- A `Select` option or action under a touch acts on its click. It acted on pointerup, so the list closed first and the click landed on whatever lay behind it.
- A `Dialog` mounted open runs its entrance, as with `v-if` and `open` set together.
- A `Popover` or a Vue `Select` inside a `Dialog` no longer closes the moment it opens. Its focus reached the dialog's trap before the overlay's own focus scope had taken over, and came straight back.

  <li>
    <strong>Sidebar variants simplified</strong> (<a href="/components/sidebar">Sidebar</a>)<br/>
    Removed unused sidebar variants (<code>floating</code>, <code>inset</code>, <code>dual-tier</code>) from type definitions, documentation, and CSS. The component now supports only <code>sidebar</code> (default) and <code>docs</code> variants.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Field horizontal orientation refactored</strong> (<a href="/components/field#horizontal-layout">Field</a>)<br/>
    Restructured HTML to use <code>LabelGroup</code> (label + description) and <code>Content</code> (control + error) containers for proper horizontal layout. Added fixed-width CSS variables <code>--field-horizontal-label-width</code> and <code>--field-horizontal-control-width</code>. Label vertical centering now uses <code>calc((control-height - label-line-height) / 2)</code> per size for pixel-perfect alignment.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Checkbox indicator aligned to Figma</strong> (<a href="/components/checkbox">Checkbox</a>)<br/>
    Indicator size changed from hardcoded 20×20px to 18×18px using the <code>--menu-checkbox-indicator-size</code> token, matching the Menu CheckboxItem and Figma specification. Border colors changed from solid grays (<code>var(--gray-200)</code>) to alpha-based tokens (<code>var(--alpha-16)</code> default, <code>var(--alpha-20)</code> hover) for consistent rendering on any background.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Checkbox label layout</strong> (<a href="/components/checkbox">Checkbox</a>)<br/>
    Label changed from <code>display: flex; align-items: center</code> to <code>display: block</code> for proper text flow in multi-line labels with descriptions. Gap tokens aligned with Menu CheckboxItem: <code>spacing(2)</code> (8px) for left orientation, <code>spacing(3)</code> (12px) for right orientation.
  </li>
</ul>

### Fixed

<ul className="changelog-list">
  <li>
    <strong>Sidebar collapse animation stability</strong> (<a href="/components/sidebar#app-shell">Sidebar</a>)<br/>
    Fixed jitter and layout shifts during sidebar collapse/expand animations. Stabilized flexbox rendering for menu items, improved padding transitions, and ensured footer cards animate in only after sidebar fully expands.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Sidebar mobile menu label visibility</strong> (<a href="/components/sidebar#mobile-menu">Sidebar</a>)<br/>
    Labels in mobile menu were sometimes hidden due to incorrect collapsed state detection. Fixed to ensure labels are always visible in mobile drawer mode regardless of desktop sidebar state.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Month stepper sizing and alignment</strong> (<a href="/components/date-range-picker#month-stepper">Date Range Picker</a>)<br/>
    Fixed multiple issues with the month stepper trigger: stepper buttons now properly scale with control size using CSS variable overrides with correct specificity; added vertical alignment for buttons; gap between buttons and text scales with control size; month text min-width now uses <code>em</code> units (9em) to scale with font size, preventing width jumps when switching between months with different name lengths (e.g., "May" vs "September"). Stepper buttons now inherit <code>pill</code> prop from parent, matching SelectControl's Clear button behavior. Added size and pill controls to Storybook docs for testing.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Clear button sizing and consistency</strong> (<a href="/components/input#clear-button">Input</a>)<br/>
    Clear button overflow and border collision at various sizes. Refactored to use built-in <code>onClear</code> prop with dedicated sizing tokens matching SelectControl's clear-iso button. Updated gutter values and added per-size offsets for proper alignment in both standard and pill modes.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>FieldError semantic color tokens</strong> (<a href="/components/field">Field</a>)<br/>
    Replaced hardcoded error colors with <code>--color-text-danger-outline</code> and <code>--color-border-danger-outline</code> semantic tokens. Removed <code>margin-top: -0.1em</code> hack from error icon, now relies on flex centering for alignment.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Pill overflow and text overlap</strong> (<a href="/components/tabs#icon-only">Tabs</a>)<br/>
    In pill mode, narrow options (icons, single digits) could be narrower than the control height, causing circular hover highlights and the active thumb to overflow or clip. Added min-width to options in pill mode ensuring they're at least as wide as the control height. Also fixed a scrollable mode regression where text items overlapped, and corrected invalid <code>white-space: wrap</code> to <code>white-space: normal</code>.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Highlight overlay radius mismatch</strong> (<a href="/components/tabs">Tabs</a>)<br/>
    The hover/active overlay (::before) used the same <code>border-radius</code> as the option, but the overlay is inset by 1px. This caused the inner rounded shape to not nest correctly. Added calculated radius as option radius minus inset, so the overlay aligns flush with the container.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Pill gutter scaling with opticallyAlign</strong> (<a href="/components/select-control#optical-alignment">Select Control</a>, <a href="/components/input#optical-alignment">Input</a>, <a href="/components/button#optical-alignment">Button</a>)<br/>
    When using <code>opticallyAlign</code> with <code>pill=&#123;true&#125;</code>, the margin calculation used only the base gutter and ignored the pill scaling multiplier (1.33×). Controls with pills now correctly multiply the margin by <code>--control-gutter-pill-scaling</code>, aligning properly with Ghost controls.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Inconsistent gap between Options and Actions</strong> (<a href="/components/select#actions">Select</a>)<br/>
    Options used <code>gap: spacing(1)</code> (4px) while Actions used <code>--menu-item-gap</code> (6px). Standardized both to use <code>--menu-item-gap</code> for consistency with the base Menu component.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Search input icon color</strong> (<a href="/components/select#grouped-options">Select</a>)<br/>
    The search icon had <code>className="fill-secondary"</code> but <code>fill="currentColor"</code> in the SVG path inherited color from the Input container, ignoring the class. Fixed by passing <code>fill="var(--color-text-secondary)"</code> as a prop with <code>!important</code> to override <code>currentColor</code>.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Missing caution color variant</strong> (<a href="/components/badge#colors--variants">Badge</a>)<br/>
    Badge was missing the <code>caution</code> color option, inconsistent with Button and Alert. Added <code>caution</code> to BadgeProps, implemented CSS styles for all variants (soft, solid, outline), and added to the Colors example matrix.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Tabs pill variant radius</strong> (<a href="/components/tabs">Tabs</a>)<br/>
    Fixed incorrect border-radius in pill mode for both segmented and vertical orientations. The option radius now correctly uses <code>--radius-full</code> when pill is active.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Field label text selection</strong> (<a href="/components/field">Field</a>)<br/>
    Removed <code>user-select: none</code> from Field label, allowing users to select and copy label text.
  </li>
</ul>
