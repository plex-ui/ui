## Unreleased

### Added

- A choice with an action at its end, as Settings › VPN: a `Field` row whose control is a radio (`RadioGroup.Item`) and that has an `action` leads with the radio's mark in the icon column. The action, such as an info button, alone stands at the row's end. On a phone the check is centred 34 from the group's edge with the words at 56; on a desktop the radio leads. `action` now places its control at the end of any row, after the row's own control, not only on a `fill` row. A tap on the row chooses; the action keeps its own click. The docs show it in Settings Forms › "VPN settings", beside "TV Provider". Styles; React and Vue.
- Groups space themselves on a phone: a `FieldGroup` after another stands as an iOS 27.2 inset list does (measured on the simulator). That is 37 from rows to rows, 60 to rows under a header (64 under a prominent one), and 49.5 past a footer of one line, 16 more for each further line; a footer then a header is 72. A `RadioGroup` around a group of its own, the group of its choices, spaces as that group. Stack the groups in a block without a gap; where a parent's gap stands between them, set `--field-group-gap` to 37px less that gap. On a desktop nothing changes. The docs show every pairing in Field › "Spacing between groups". Styles.
- Action rows in a `FieldGroup`: a ghost `Button` with `block` in a row is the row itself, as an iOS 27.2 list draws a button (measured on the simulator). It takes the row's height and label type (52 high, 17/22 on a phone), and its words start at the row's inset (20 on a phone) with no chevron. An icon after the words stands at the trailing edge, such as an arrow for a link that leaves the app. `color` tints the words: `info` for the accent, `danger` for a destructive row. A row with `data-align="center"` centres its words. The docs show it in Field › "Action rows". Styles; React and Vue.
- `reach` on `Sortable`: where a drag from the list goes. `group`, the default, is as before: the lists of the group and its targets. `list` keeps the items in the list, ordered by hand, and hands them to a `Sortable.Target` (Vue `SortableTarget`) of the group. `targets` hands them only to a target: the order stays, so no line shows and nothing parts, a drop anywhere else puts the item back, and the arrow keys leave it. A list that is not `group` neither gives items to the other lists of the group nor takes theirs. The docs show it in "Onto a folder". React and Vue.
- `ZoomIn` and `ZoomOut` icons: the magnifier of `Search` with a plus or a minus inside, both 7 of 24 long and 2 thick. React and Vue.
- `--alpha-03`, 3% of the alpha base, between `--alpha-02` and `--alpha-04`. A full row's hover with a mouse takes it: `--field-row-hover-background`, `--alpha-03` light and `--alpha-05` dark, the light fill of a list row rather than a button's 8%.
- `handlePlacement` on `Sortable` (`start` or `end`): where a shown grip stands in its row on every width. Without it the grip starts the row on a desktop and ends it on a phone, as before.
- `icon` on a `Field` row (Vue `#icon` slot): a symbol or an app's picture before the label, as an iOS cell's image. The label, its description and the line above the row start after it: a 28px column and 8px to the text on a phone (the text at 56, as Settings lists apps), 32px and 12px on a desktop. An `img` takes an app icon's corners, an icon the symbol size. Tokens `--field-row-icon-size`, `--field-row-icon-gap`, `--field-row-icon-symbol-size`, `--field-row-icon-radius`, `--field-row-icon-color`.
- `confirmClose` on `Dialog.Content` (Vue `confirm-close`): a dialog with unsaved changes asks before it closes. Close, `Dialog.Close`, Escape, a press outside and a phone sheet pulled down all ask; only the answer that discards closes it. On a phone Sheet the question stands at the bar's Close as an iOS 27 confirmation, measured on the 27.2 simulator: a glass popover 240 wide over the Close, with no dimming, the question in 17/22 over a full-width 48px red pill, and a tap outside keeps editing. On a desktop it is an alert, the question as its title, with Keep Editing and Discard Changes (`confirmLabel`, `cancelLabel`); both stand over a dialog with its own `zIndex`. The docs show it in "Ask before discarding". React and Vue.
- `Popover.Anchor` (Vue `PopoverAnchor`): stands a popover at any element that is not its trigger, such as a bar button with its own action; a press on that element is not outside the popover, and focus returns to it. React and Vue.
- `zIndex` and `aria-labelledby` on `Popover.Content` (Vue `z-index`, `aria-labelledby`): a popover over a layer above 50, and the element that names it. Token `--popover-z-index`. React and Vue.
- `startAdornment` and `endAdornment` on `Slider` (Vue slots): text or an icon at the ends of a horizontal track, as Settings puts a small and a large letter at the ends of a text size slider. Hidden from assistive technology; tokens `--slider-adornment-*`. The Overview shows them under `adornments`. React and Vue.

### Fixed

- `Button` with `uniform`, `pill` and `opticallyAlign` now moves its icon to the edge it is aligned to. The pill's gutter rule took a uniform pill back to its zero gutter, so the button stood its full circle in from the edge. Styles.
- `EmptyMessage` on a phone (`touch.css`) is iOS's ContentUnavailableView, measured on the iOS 27.2 simulator: the symbol bare, without its circle, 48px in the secondary label, 19px above a 20/25 bold title, the description 17/25 right under it, 32px insets, the block centred in the free space. Actions stand 28px under the text at their own width, a 28px capsule with a 15px label and 12px gutters, the next 14px below, instead of 50px buttons across the bottom of a filled screen. New tokens `--empty-message-icon-background` and `--empty-message-icon-secondary-color`.
- `FieldGroup` on a desktop: a full-row action (a ghost `block` button in a row) fills the row to its edges when pressed instead of shrinking with gaps at its sides, and its hover and press keep the card's corners on the first and last rows.
- `Field` choice and popup rows take the pointer on every width, not only on a phone, and with a mouse the row fills on hover with the card's corners at the ends: the whole row is the control's target, as it was already for clicks.
- `FieldGroup`: a row right after a `Sortable` list, or a list right after a row, is parted from it by the row line, on a phone and a desktop alike (an "Add …" row at the end of a sorted list stood without one). A full-row button in the first or last row of a list that is not at the card's edge no longer rounds its pressed surface.
- A `Tabs` badge is hidden only on a phone: with `touch.css` below 768px for segments and underline tabs, or in a phone layout (`data-plex-layout="phone"`). A page that is only narrow, without `touch.css`, keeps its badges; 0.47.0 hid them on any page under 768px. React and Vue.
- On a phone the lists of `Select` and `Menu` draw no scrollbar, as an iOS list draws none as it opens: a browser flashed its overlay scrollbar along the list's edge for a second on every open. They still scroll.
- Opening a menu or a dialog no longer restyles the whole page twice. Selectors that reached any element after a `:has()` or through `* ~ *` and `* + *` made each node added to the page, such as a focus guard, restyle every element; they now name what they style (`ButtonGroup`, `AvatarGroup`, `MarkdownEditor`, `Toast`, `Dialog`, `Alert`, `Sortable`, `Badge`, `Sidebar`, `EmptyMessage`). The styles they give are the same, but for one: a glass `ButtonGroup` on a phone sizes the kit's buttons (`Button`, `ButtonLink`, `CopyButton`, which carry `data-variant`), not a plain `<button>` put in it.

- `Toolbar` and `Page` on a phone: without a top safe area (a page in a browser) a top bar keeps above its row the air it keeps below it, so its controls stand in the middle of the bar: 10px and a 64px bar, or 14px and 72px when the bar holds a search field; it kept the 16px gutter. Under a status bar it stays below it, and a Sheet's top bar keeps its 16px. A full-screen dialog's top bar too. React and Vue.

## 0.48.0 (2026-11-14)

The glass row control ships in `@plexui/styles` 0.7.0. `@plexui/ui` 0.48.0 and `@plexui/vue` 0.28.0 need `^0.7.0`, since the row's 40px glass control is set by the 0.7 styles; earlier ui and vue versions keep the 0.6 styles they were built with. `@plexui/mcp` is 0.2.23.

### Changed

- A `SegmentedControl` in a phone `Toolbar.Row` (Vue `ToolbarRow`) is glass at its default size, as a chat list draws its folders under its bar in iOS 27: a 40px capsule of the large control's glass, the gray selection 34px and 3px inside it, 15px Medium labels 12px from their segments' ends. `size="sm"` gives the small plain control (32px) it was before, `size="lg"` the large glass one (48px), and `mobileMaterial="flat"` the small plain one. React and Vue.

## 0.47.0 (2026-11-13)

The phone segmented control changes in `@plexui/styles` 0.6.0. `@plexui/ui` 0.47.0 and `@plexui/vue` 0.27.0 need `^0.6.0`, since `mobileSegmentWidth` and the narrowing air are set by the 0.6 styles; earlier ui and vue versions keep the 0.5 styles they were built with. `@plexui/mcp` is 0.2.22.

### Added

- `mobileSegmentWidth` on `SegmentedControl` (Vue `mobile-segment-width`): the widths of a phone's segments, as UISegmentedControl sets them. `equal`, the default, gives every segment the widest one's width, and `content` gives each its label and the same air around it, a compact control. Either way, segments that do not fit take their labels' widths. React and Vue.

### Changed

- `SegmentedControl` on a phone draws no badges, as iOS segments carry none. A badge's count stays in the segment's accessible name, so write it into the label to show it. React and Vue.
- The small phone segmented control (`sm` and smaller, with `touch.css`) sets its labels 9px inside their segments instead of 6px, as iOS 27.2 sets the widest label of a 32pt control at its own width (9–10pt). Where labels at their widths still do not fit, the air beside each narrows by its share, to 4px, before the segments scroll, as UIKit narrows a squeezed control's (`--tabs-segment-gutter`, set by script). The large control keeps 4px. React and Vue.

## 0.46.0 (2026-11-13)

The phone changes ship in `@plexui/styles` 0.5.0. `@plexui/ui` 0.46.0 and `@plexui/vue` 0.26.0 need `^0.5.0`, since the reset of a phone's `Slider` is now a kit button that the 0.4 styles would stretch to 44px; earlier ui and vue versions keep the 0.4 styles they were built with. `@plexui/mcp` is 0.2.21.

### Added

- `MinusLg` icon in React and Vue: the minus of `PlusLg`, its bar 18 of 24 wide with a 2px stroke. On a phone a plus is `PlusLg` and a minus `MinusLg`, with `iconSize="sm"` in a glass button, the plus of an iOS 27.2 bar (about 18px of ink).
- `ButtonGroup` on a phone with `touch.css`: a group that holds a field comes apart, as iOS 27.2 never joins a field and its buttons. With `mobileMaterial="glass"`, floating over the content as the composer of Messages, the field is a 40px glass capsule (16px gutters, 17px text) and each button beside it a 40px glass circle, 12px apart; in a `Toolbar` or `Toolbar.Row` they take the bar's size and gap. A select among them is the glass capsule of a bar's pull-down button (17px Medium, 16px gutters). An invalid field or select keeps its red line over the glass, a button inside the field keeps its 44px target without growing it, and a group that holds an `InputGroup` stays joined. New tokens `--button-group-glass-field-size`, `--button-group-glass-field-gap` and `--button-group-glass-field-gutter` (`40px`, `12px`, `16px`). React and Vue.

### Changed

- A filled, tinted or gray button on a phone with `touch.css` sets its label in Medium (510) instead of Semibold, and a plain (`ghost`) one keeps Regular, as the iOS 27 kit draws its text buttons on glass and Apple's apps set the pair apart. Measured at @3x in the iOS 27.2 simulator, an Apple app's filled label strokes 1.93pt: Inter 510 strokes 1.85pt, 590 2.12pt. The actions of a phone `Alert` follow it. The round letters of a `ToggleGroup` (a clock's days) stay Semibold (`--toggle-group-round-font-weight`, `590`).
- `Slider` on a phone (narrower than 768px): the reset is the kit's small round gray button, a soft secondary `uniform` `2xs` button, a 28px circle with an 18px glyph and a 44px target, 8px after the value, instead of a bare glyph on a 44px square. The value field is as wide as its digits, 44px high. A hidden reset keeps its place, so the value does not move. A desktop keeps the 24px ghost reset. `touch.css` no longer sets `--slider-reset-size`, `--slider-reset-icon-size` and `--slider-reset-color`; the first two still size a desktop reset. React and Vue.
- `SegmentedControl` on a phone: the segments share one width, the widest label's, as iOS 27.2 draws a segmented control at its own width; where that does not fit, each segment takes its label with the same air around it, as iOS sizes segments by their content, and segments that still do not fit scroll inside the capsule. A `block` control keeps equal shares of its row. The widest label is measured by script and set as the grid track (`--tabs-segment-track`), since a grid that hugs its content sizes its tracks by the sum of its labels. React and Vue.

### Fixed

- `Slider` on a touch screen no longer stays pressed after the finger lifts: the press ends when the pointer is lifted or cancelled anywhere on the page and when the slider loses its pointer capture, as it does in a sheet that scrolls, not only when the slider hears it. React and Vue.
- A vertical glass `ButtonGroup` on a phone (zoom controls over a canvas): each button takes its share of the gap and of the padding, so its pressed shape fills its part of the capsule and the first and last are as round as its ends, instead of a 48px circle 4px off the capsule's end. The symbols stay where they were.

## 0.45.0 (2026-11-13)

The phone bars change in `@plexui/styles` 0.4.0: `Toolbar.Row`, the `blur` background, iOS 27.2's soft and hard edges and a large title that scrolls with the content. `@plexui/ui` 0.45.0 and `@plexui/vue` 0.25.0 need `^0.4.0`, and earlier ui and vue versions keep the 0.3 styles they were built with, since a large title is now laid out in `Page.Content`. `@plexui/mcp` is 0.2.20.

### Added

- `Toolbar.Row` (Vue `ToolbarRow`): a row under the controls that is part of the bar, for a segmented control that switches the screen's views, as a chat list shows its folders under its bar, or a search field. It spans the bar between its gutters, 10px under the controls (`--toolbar-row-gap`), and the bar's background and line take it in; several rows stand in their order, 10px apart, so a search field can stand over a segmented control, as iOS 27.2 sets them. A `SegmentedControl` in it is the small plain one at its default size (32px, as iOS 27.2 draws a segmented Picker under a bar) and fills the row; its segments scroll when they do not fit, and `size="lg"` gives the large glass one. A search field in it is the bar's. Under `titleStyle="large"` on a page the rows stand under the title at the top of `Page.Content` and, scrolled up to the bar, stay pinned under its controls, the bar's background reaching under them. The Overview of the Toolbar page has `rows` and `size (row)` controls. React and Vue.
- `background="blur"` for every bar of a phone screen (`Toolbar` at the top and the bottom, `Page.Header`, `Page.Footer`, the bars of a Sheet and `TabBar`), in React and Vue: no fill and no line, only the content under the bar blurred, a little over the bar and 32px past it and heavily toward the screen's edge (measured on a reference screen: text smeared out 40px inside the bar's edge and clear 27px past it). It shows once content is under the bar, as hard and glass do. Tokens `--page-bar-blur-light` and `--page-bar-blur-heavy` (`3px`, `12px`), placed by `--page-bar-blur-top-mask`, `--page-bar-blur-top-heavy-mask`, `--page-bar-blur-bottom-mask` and `--page-bar-blur-bottom-heavy-mask`. The `background` controls of the Toolbar, Page, Dialog and Tab Bar pages offer it.

### Changed

- An `Input` with `type="search"` and `variant="soft"` in the content of a phone screen, under a large title or at the top of a sheet, is the bar's search field: the same magnifier and text position and, with `touch.css`, the same glass capsule (`--mobile-glass-*`), so a search field looks the same wherever it stands, instead of the flat gray fill (iOS 27 draws that one flat; one look was chosen). An invalid one keeps its red outline. React and Vue.
- `background="solid"` is the earlier name of `"hard"` on every bar: iOS 27.2 has two scroll edges, soft and hard, and no opaque bar without a line, and a hard bar draws its line only once content is under it, so at rest the two look the same. The four backgrounds are `soft`, `hard`, `glass` and `blur`; `BarBackground` lists them, and `"solid"` stays accepted (`BarBackgroundAlias`). A bottom `Toolbar` with `blur` stands on the content as a soft one does, with no band.

### Fixed

- A bottom `Toolbar` keeps its items in place whatever its `background`: with `glass` or `hard` they no longer move out to the bar's gutter (16px, 20px from 414px) and stay 28px from the sides; the band only starts 10px above them. iOS 27.2 stands them at the same place over a soft and a hard edge (28px from the sides and above the home indicator). React and Vue.
- `background="soft"` on a bottom `Toolbar` and on `Page.Footer` of a page draws iOS 27.2's bottom scroll edge instead of nothing: once content runs on under the bar, a blur hides it under the controls behind a veil of the canvas color, none 34px above the items, 15% at their top and 46% from 43px into them down to the screen's edge. New tokens `--page-bar-soft-bottom-reach`, `--page-bar-soft-bottom-veil` and `--page-bar-soft-bottom-blur-mask`. A Sheet's bottom bar keeps its own edge, and `TabBar` keeps none, as iOS 27.2 runs the content clear under a tab bar. React and Vue.
- The line of a `hard` bar is a hairline, as iOS 27.2 draws it: one device pixel, black 10% in light, instead of 1px at 12%, three times as thick on an iPhone. `--page-bar-hard-line` is now `var(--page-bar-line)`.
- `Toolbar`, `Page.Header` and the header of a Sheet on a phone: `background="soft"` at the top is the soft scroll edge of iOS 27.2 instead of an opaque band of the canvas color. Once content is under the bar, a blur hides it under the row of controls and lets go of it past the bar, under a thin veil of the canvas color: 55% at the top, 20% at the bar's bottom, none 24px below. At rest it draws nothing, so the first rows and a large title stand clear. New tokens `--page-bar-soft-veil`, `--page-bar-soft-blur` and `--page-bar-soft-blur-mask`. React and Vue.
- `Toolbar` with `titleStyle="large"` on a page: the title stands at the top of `Page.Content` and scrolls with the content, overscroll included, instead of being moved by script a frame or two behind it. Once it has gone under the bar, the inline title comes in over 0.3s (`--toolbar-large-title-swap-duration`), and a hard or glass bar shows its line with it, as iOS 27.2 swaps them; the swap no longer follows the scroll. `Page` no longer writes `--page-scroll-y`, and `--page-top` ends under the row of controls. React and Vue.
- A large title with a subtitle on a phone stands where it stands without one: a subtitle that comes or goes no longer moves the title 3px, only the content under it.

## 0.44.0 (2026-11-12)

The kit is set in Inter, with Geist Mono for code, on the desktop and on a phone, both shipped with `@plexui/styles`; text tracks by the Plex UI Figma kit's table. `@plexui/styles` is 0.3.0, since the face and its tracking change how every text sets: `@plexui/ui` 0.44.0 and `@plexui/vue` 0.24.0 need `^0.3.0` (they import its font stylesheets), and earlier ui and vue versions keep the 0.2 styles they were built with. `@plexui/mcp` is 0.2.16.

### Changed

- `Toolbar` on a phone: a page's bottom bar on a band (`glass`, `hard` or `solid`) is the top bar turned over: 10px above its items, its side items at `--toolbar-gutter` (16px, 20px from 414px) where the top bar's corner items and a grouped list's rows stand, and that gutter or the home indicator's inset less 6px under them. It kept the 4px and 28px of a bar with no band (`soft`), which stays as it was, as do a Sheet's bar and a single wide action.
- The kit is set in **Inter 4**, on the desktop as in the Plex UI Figma kit and on a phone too (owner 12.11): the system stack showed Apple's San Francisco on Apple devices only, and San Francisco may not be shipped on the web. `@plexui/styles` ships Inter's variable files with the optical-size axis (`inter.css`, 14 woff2 split by script, SIL Open Font License), imported by `@plexui/ui/css` and `@plexui/styles/css`. Tracking follows each text's size as the Plex UI Figma kit sets Inter, its Android & Web typography mode (`--font-sans-tracking`, set on every element in the base layer: -0.10px at 14px, -0.15px at 17 and 22px, -0.20px from 28 to 48px); monospace text keeps its face's spacing. `--font-tracking-wide`, `-normal` and `-tight` read it. Components that left tracking to the system font take it too.
- `--font-sans` and `--font-mono` are declared once, in `variables-tokens-root.css`; `variables-tailwind-theme.css` no longer repeats them.
- `EmptyMessage` on a phone takes the Plex UI Figma kit's empty state in Inter (Content Card and its screens): the symbol in a 64px slot 24px above the text, a 22/28 bold title, 4px, a 17/22 regular description and 40px insets, and 50px buttons with a 17px Medium label, a plain one included (it was the iOS 27 kit's SF sizes: 22px description, 52px buttons). In a list or a card the buttons follow 24px under the text, one at its own width and two across the width 12px apart; a message that fills a screen keeps them across its bottom, as iOS 27 does, the text centred above. The Overview's second action is a plain button, the kit's Tertiary. The Sidebar demo's workflow step "Selfie verification" is now "Selfie check", which keeps to one line in the phone drawer with the kit's tracking.
- Monospace text (code, ids, `font-mono`) is set in **Geist Mono**, the code face of the Plex UI Figma kit, instead of the system's monospace (SF Mono on Apple devices): `@plexui/styles` ships its variable files (`geist-mono.css`, 12 woff2 split by script, SIL Open Font License) as `--font-mono`.
- `DatePicker` month arrows on a phone and the docs' preview keyboard (shift, delete, return) are drawn with Plex UI and Lucide icons instead of outlines of Apple's symbols.
- `Toast`: the close button stands at the trailing end of the toast, inside it, as the one trailing item of an iOS notification banner: centred on a toast without a description, level with the title beside one, 12px from the edge with 8px before it; on a touch screen its 44px target is the trailing top corner. A toast with a close button keeps 40px for it (56px on a touch screen). Vue's `closeButtonPosition` no longer moves it. The 8px before an action button is on its leading side in a right-to-left toaster too.
- Vue `toast` takes `icon: null`, which leaves a typed toast's icon out, as React's does; its options are exported as `ToastOptions`.
- `Dialog` on a phone (React and Vue) is always an iOS presentation: without `mobileVariant` it is a Sheet (it was a centred card of the web), and `mobilePlacement="center"` makes an Alert. A desktop is unchanged.
- `Dialog` Sheet footer on a phone: the main action stands on top and a soft, outline or ghost action beside a solid one stands under it as a plain button in the iOS tint (red for danger), as an iOS setup sheet lays out Continue and its second choice. Write the footer as before, with the main action last.
- `Dialog` Alert on a phone: the solid primary action takes the accent fill and white label of the iOS 27 kit's Default role, as a sheet's prominent action does.
- `Dialog` Sheet pulled down on a phone asks `onInteractOutside` (`@interact-outside`) first, as a press outside does: `event.preventDefault()` keeps it open and it returns to its place. The event is a `dialog.sheetDrag` CustomEvent with the touch as `detail.originalEvent`.

### Added

- `--list-row-inset`: how far a row's content stands inside its group, on every width: 16px, 20px on a phone with the touch stylesheet (it was `--mobile-list-inset`, set on a phone only, which stays as its alias). Field rows, the line between rows and a group's title and footer take it; an app's own rows in a FieldGroup take it too, to line up with the kit's.
- Tailwind utilities `px-list-inset` and `px-layout-margin` (and every spacing utility: `pl-`, `mx-`, `gap-`) for `--list-row-inset` and the edge of a screen or a sheet (`--page-gutter`: 16px, 20px on a phone from 414px).
- `--color-accent`, the accent of the kit in one variable (`#0285ff` by default): primary links, focus rings and the iOS tint (the prominent bar button, a phone Dialog's main and tinted actions, an alert's preferred action, `IndexBar` letters, the `Sortable` drop line) follow it, with its steps drawn from it in both themes (`--accent-50` to `--accent-700`, `--accent-a25` to `--accent-a75`, `--color-text-accent`, `--color-background-accent-solid`, `--color-background-accent-soft`). Status colors keep theirs. See [Colors](/docs/foundations/colors#accent) and [Typography](/docs/foundations/typography#typeface).
- `side` on `Dialog.HeaderAction` (`DialogHeaderAction` in Vue): `"leading"` puts the action at the start of the header, and on a phone in the Sheet's bar in place of Close, as the back button of a screen pushed inside an iOS sheet. `DialogHeaderActionProps` is exported.

## 0.43.0 (2026-11-11)

Most of this release is the phone (with `touch.css`, under 768px or inside a `PhoneLayout`): about 25 components are measured again in the iOS 27.2 simulator on an iPhone 17 (402pt), against UIKit, SwiftUI and Apple's apps, and take its sizes. A desktop is unchanged unless an entry says so. `@plexui/styles` is 0.2.0, since it renames and removes tokens and the Stat Card stylesheet: `@plexui/ui` 0.43.0 and `@plexui/vue` 0.23.0 need `^0.2.0`, and earlier ui and vue versions keep the 0.1 styles they were built with.

### Added

- `TabBar` tab badges and a bottom accessory (React and Vue). `badge` on `TabBar.Item` (a prop of `TabBarItem` in Vue) is the iOS tab badge: an 18px capsule of 12px regular text in the danger solid color at the top of the tab, starting just past its middle, read after the label ("Calendar 3"). 0, an empty text and nothing show no badge, as iOS hides `.badge(0)`, and a minimized bar keeps the badge of its selected tab. `accessory` (the `accessory` slot in Vue) is a 48px glass capsule across the bar, 8px above the tabs; minimized, it stands between the two circles. With an accessory the bar is 151px tall in both states, so the `Page` under it does not move. New tokens `--tab-bar-badge-*`, `--tab-bar-minimized-badge-*`, `--tab-bar-accessory-height`, `--tab-bar-accessory-gap` and `--tab-bar-minimized-accessory-gap`.
- `noneLabel` on `Select` (`none-label` in Vue, default "None"): in a window under 768px a clearable `Select` with its default trigger has no clear button and unsets its value with a None row, first in its list and in a section of its own, as an iOS pop-up button does (Reminders' Early Reminder). The row is checked while nothing is chosen, choosing it closes the list (a multiple list too), and a custom trigger keeps its own clearing. The desktop is unchanged.
- `Input type="search"` on a phone is the search field of iOS 27.2 in the content (Files under a large title, Settings at the top of a sheet): the magnifier and text of a `Toolbar` search field, and with `variant="soft" pill` its fill and capsule, 44px tall. - `mobileMaterial` on `SegmentedControl` (`Tabs`; `mobile-material` in Vue): `"flat"` gives the large phone control the plain look (49px), `"glass"` gives the small one glass. The default is glass at md and larger and flat at sm and smaller; a desktop ignores it.

## 0.42.0 (2026-11-09)

### Changed

- `Slider` disabled on a desktop looks disabled, as the macOS 27 kit draws it: the fill takes the track's color and both lighten to 60% of the track, the fill a shade darker where it lies over the track, and the thumb's ring lightens with the fill. ### Fixed

- `dateTimeCell` failed to hydrate on a page rendered on a server (React error 418, a Vue mismatch) for a reader outside UTC, and in Safari for every reader: the server wrote the time in its own zone, and WebKit words a date differently from Node ("Jan 15, 2023 at 09:00 AM"). Offsets are read the same in every engine (`+02`, `-0530`). A value that is no date (`2023-02-30`, an hour of 25) shows as it is instead of throwing, and a date written another way (`Jan 15, 2023 09:00`) is written as it is on the server and read in the reader's browser.

## 0.40.0 (2026-11-09)

### Changed

- `MarkdownEditor` with `formats` that leave something out: pasted text keeps the markers of a format left out as text, as if they were typed, and so do headings, quotes, code, rules, tables and images, which the editor never offers: `- one` pasted where lists are left out is the line `- one`, `*it*` where italic is left out stays `*it*`. ### Fixed

- `MarkdownEditor` writes the `&` of what markdown reads as a character reference as `&amp;`, so typed `&lt;` or `&copy;` stays those characters when its markdown is read again instead of turning into `<` or `©`.
- `MarkdownEditor` escapes a run of `_` unless it is inside a word, so typed `__s__` reads back as that text, not an italic `s` between two underscores.
- `MarkdownEditor` writes the URL of a link shown as its own address (`<https://...>`) as it is: an `_`, `~` or `*` in it was escaped, and the backslash ended up in the address when read again.
- `MarkdownEditor` escapes a line of `|`, `:` and `-` after a line break, which would turn the line above it into the head of a table.
- `MarkdownEditor` does not write white space of four columns or more at the start of a paragraph, which read back as code, its escapes showing. Markdown drops white space there anyway.
- `MarkdownEditor` writes a line ending in a link's title as `&#10;`, so a title over two lines reads back as the same link even when its second line would start a block.

- `Breadcrumb`: the trail of pages above a page title. A `nav` landmark around an ordered list; `Breadcrumb.Item` is a link (`href` or `to`, through the `linkComponent` of `PlexUIProvider`), a button (`onClick`), a plain step or the `current` page (`aria-current="page"`). - `Checkbox` and `RadioGroup.Item` take a `description`: a second line under the label, outside it, linked through `aria-describedby`. `Switch` already had one.
- `Menu.Item` and `Menu.Link` take `danger` for destructive actions: the label and the highlight use the danger color.
- `EmptyMessage` takes `size="sm"` for an empty panel, card section or list, and `align="start"` for an empty state under a section heading.
- `Alert` takes `banner`: a full-width strip with square corners and lines above and below only, for offline, read-only or new-version notices.
- `Input` takes `variant="ghost"`: no border and no fill, for a field its container already frames, such as a search line at the top of a dialog.
- `FieldError` takes `icon={false}` for a message that stands alone, such as under a centered code input.
- `CopyButton` and `CopyTooltip` report the clipboard result through `onCopy(copied)` (Vue: the `copy` event).
