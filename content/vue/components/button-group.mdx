<UsageBlock>{`import { ButtonGroup, ButtonGroupSeparator, ButtonGroupText } from "@plexui/vue/components/ButtonGroup";`}</UsageBlock>

Wraps any row of controls (`Button`, `Input`, a popover trigger, ...) and removes the shared border so they read as one unit. Nest `ButtonGroup` inside itself to create visually separated sub-groups (the outer group automatically spaces them).

On a phone with `touch.css` a group of buttons in a [`Toolbar`](/vue/components/toolbar) is the Button Group of iOS: one glass capsule holding its buttons, the same as `ToolbarGroup`. `mobile-material="glass"` asks for the capsule outside a toolbar, for controls that float over the content. iOS has no joined buttons in the content, so a group there keeps its joined look at the touch sizes. A field among glass buttons comes apart from them, each a glass shape of its own, as the composer of iOS Messages. The Phone view of each example shows the group where iOS puts it, in a bar or floating at the bottom of the screen, or the screen iOS builds where it has no such group.

## API

### ButtonGroup

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `orientation` | `"horizontal" \| "vertical"` | `"horizontal"` | Direction of the joined items |
| `mobile-material` | `"glass" \| "flat"` | | Phone surface with `touch.css`: `"glass"` makes the group one glass capsule outside a `Toolbar`, and a group that holds a field comes apart into glass shapes; `"flat"` keeps the joined buttons in one |
| `class` | `string` | | Applied to the root `<div>` |

Renders a `<div role="group">`. All other attributes and listeners fall through to it.

### ButtonGroupSeparator

Inherits from [`Separator`](/vue/components/separator). `orientation` defaults to `"vertical"` (the axis that looks right between horizontal items); pass an explicit value inside a `ButtonGroup orientation="vertical"`.

### ButtonGroupText

Renders a muted label pill that slots into the group alongside buttons. Use it for prefixes ("@", "https://"), currency hints, or any non-interactive label that should share the joined shape.

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| `as-child` | `boolean` | `false` | Render the slotted child (e.g. a `<label>` tied to an input) instead of a `<div>` |
| `class` | `string` | | Applied to the wrapper |

## Styling

Nesting `ButtonGroup` inside a parent `ButtonGroup` automatically introduces a gap between the sub-groups. The group-specific tokens:

- `--button-group-nested-gap`: space between sibling sub-groups (defaults to `spacing(2)` = 8px)
- `--button-group-separator-color`: color of the `ButtonGroupSeparator` line
- `--button-group-text-background` / `--button-group-text-color` / `--button-group-text-border-color`: surface, text, and 1px inset border of `ButtonGroupText`
- `--button-group-text-font-size` / `--button-group-text-font-weight`: typography of `ButtonGroupText`
- `--button-group-glass-item-size`, `--button-group-glass-gap`, `--button-group-glass-padding`: a glass group on a phone, its buttons, the space between them and at the ends (default `44px`, `10px`, `1px`; in a `Toolbar` the bar's own)
- `--button-group-glass-vertical-item-size`, `--button-group-glass-vertical-gap`, `--button-group-glass-vertical-padding`: a vertical glass group (default `48px`, `6px`, `4px`)
- `--button-group-glass-nested-gap`, `--button-group-glass-label-gutter`: between glass groups side by side, and the gutter of a label in one (default `10px`, `16px`)
- `--button-group-glass-field-size`, `--button-group-glass-field-gap`, `--button-group-glass-field-gutter`: a glass group that holds a field and floats over the content, its field and buttons, the space between them and the field's text gutter (default `40px`, `12px`, `16px`; in a `Toolbar` the bar's size and gap)

Radius, border, and focus styles of joined buttons come from each child control's own tokens (`--button-radius`, `--input-radius`, etc.); the group only strips the shared edge radii so joined items align.
