On Phone, each slider stands in a cell of a grouped list, as in iOS Settings: the group's title names the slider and the slider fills the cell. One value ends the cell, a range goes under the track, and with marks the cell shows the scale instead of a value. For a slider among other settings, see [Settings with a slider](/vue/components/settings-forms#settings-with-a-slider).

Typing in the value input reports the parsed value after 250ms; Enter, Escape or leaving the input commits it right away, and Home, End and the arrow keys (Shift for ten steps) change it at once. The input accepts a minus sign when `min` is below zero. Inside a `<form>`, a hidden input per thumb fires an `input` event when its value changes.

## Styling

On a phone the slider follows the iOS 27 kit: the thumb of a horizontal slider turns into a clear lens of glass over the track while it is pressed (of a range, the thumb nearest the press), and a disabled slider shows at half opacity. On a desktop a disabled slider follows the macOS 27 kit: the accent leaves it, so its fill takes the track's color and both lighten, the fill a shade darker where it lies over the track, and the thumb keeps its face.

Slider reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--slider-range-color`: range color (default `light-dark(var(--gray-450), var(--gray-600))`)
- `--slider-track-color`: track color (default `light-dark(var(--gray-150), var(--gray-400))`)
