## Sharing settings

One choice checked in a group of its own, switches under headers, and a row in the accent, as AirDrop lays out who may send. The choice is a `RadioGroup` around its `FieldGroup`: on a phone the chosen row carries the check, on a desktop the radio. Its footer ends with a link, a primary `TextLink` in the footer's text. Each switch stands under a header with a footer that says what it does. Manage Known Contacts is a ghost `Button` with `block` in a row, the accent with no chevron, with its footer under it. The groups space themselves as an iOS list, the wrapped choice too.
