  />

## Examples

### Overview

<ComponentPreview language="vue" details={<>Bind the range (<code>[start, end]</code> luxon <code>DateTime</code>s, or <code>null</code>) with <code>v-model:value</code>. The <code>@change</code> event carries the same range and, when a shortcut was picked, that shortcut as its second argument, like React's <code>onChange(range, shortcut)</code>.</>} code={overview} previewMinHeight={168}>
  <VueDateRangePickerDemo template={overview} />
</ComponentPreview>

### Limits

<ComponentPreview language="vue" details={<>Use <code>min</code> and <code>max</code> to limit the selectable range. Use <code>max-range-days</code> to limit the range length. Here <code>minDate</code> is 30 days ago (start of day) and <code>maxDate</code> is the end of today.</>} code={limits} previewMinHeight={168}>
  <VueDateRangePickerDemo template={limits} initialRange="limits" />
</ComponentPreview>

### Shortcuts

<ComponentPreview language="vue" details={<>Use the <code>shortcuts</code> array to provide quick date range options. When a shortcut is selected, its label is shown in the trigger. Each shortcut has a <code>label</code> and a <code>getDateRange()</code> function; <code>dayShortcuts</code> here holds Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days and Last 30 days.</>} code={shortcuts} previewMinHeight={168}>
  <VueDateRangePickerDemo template={shortcuts} />
</ComponentPreview>

### Month stepper

<ComponentPreview language="vue" details={<>Enable a stepper in the trigger with <code>trigger-stepper-unit="month"</code>. When the selected range matches the unit, the trigger shows the stepper.</>} code={monthStepper} previewMinHeight={168}>
  <VueDateRangePickerDemo template={monthStepper} initialRange="month" stageClassName="" controls={[{ name: "size", type: "select", options: SIZES, default: "lg" }, { name: "pill", type: "boolean", default: true }]} />
</ComponentPreview>
