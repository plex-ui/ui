On Phone, put a date range picker in a row of a `FieldGroup`, as the [Form Examples](/vue/components/form-examples) do. In the row the range and its up-down chevrons end at the row's edge, as a pop-up, and open the calendar. The row is its frame and sets its height, insets and type, so the phone examples leave out `size`, `pill` and `clearable`, set `:trigger-show-icon="false"`, show short dates (`trigger-date-format="MMM d"`) and None when there is no range. In a row the month of a month stepper keeps the width of the longest month, so its arrows stay put when the month changes.

## API

In the calendars, the arrow keys move focus by a day or a week, Home and End move it to the first or last day of the week row, and Page Up and Page Down move it by a month (January 31 goes to the last day of February). Focus moves across the visible months, and a day past them turns the calendars by a month first. Focus stays put when the day it would move to is disabled: outside `min` and `max`, or past `max-range-days` while a range is being picked. Enter and Space pick the focused day.

## Styling

Date Range Picker reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--input-border-color-invalid`: border color invalid (default `var(--color-border-danger-outline)`)
- `--input-outline-border-color`: outline border color (default `var(--color-border-primary-outline)`)
- `--input-outline-border-color-hover`: outline border color hover (default `light-dark(var(--alpha-25), var(--alpha-30))`)
- `--input-placeholder-text-color`: placeholder text color (default `var(--color-text-tertiary)`)
- `--input-text-color`: text color (default `var(--color-text)`)
- `--select-control-font-weight`: font weight (default `var(--font-weight-normal)`)
