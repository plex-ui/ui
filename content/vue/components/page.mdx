## Phone

A page needs a bounded parent. These demos give it a 420px frame that fills the preview on desktop, and the full viewport on phones:

```css
.page-frame {
  height: 420px;
  overflow: hidden;
}

@media (max-width: 767px) {
  .page-frame {
    height: 100dvh;
  }
}
```

Under 768px the header contains a 44px toolbar row with 10px below it, below the device top safe area, or the page gutter from the window's top where there is none (a page in a browser). It floats over the content and meets what scrolls under it by its `background`, see [Overview](#overview): by default it is a translucent, blurred bar with a hairline once content is under it. With `background="soft"`, once content is under the bar, a blur hides it under the row and lets go of it below, under a thin veil of the canvas color, and a bar without a title, of controls alone, covers only the status bar and leaves the content clear under its controls, as native iOS 26 does. The content keeps its place through `--page-top`, the top inset and the bar together, also for anything else that has to start below the bar, such as a banner. A large Toolbar title stands at the top of `Page.Content` and scrolls with it; the content starts 16px under its baseline.

Keep the row to a title and one group of actions. The bar has no room for a second row, so anything set under `PageBar`, such as a search field, belongs at the top of `PageContent` on a phone. `useBreakpoint("md")` tells which one to render. Use `PageLeading` for a Back button or the menu button of a sidebar. The title stays centred between the leading and trailing controls.

For a phone bar of its own, with a large title, a search field, a button group or a bottom bar, put a [Toolbar](/vue/components/toolbar) in the page. Under 768px it replaces `PageHeader` and `PageFooter`, which stay the desktop header and footer.

Use it inside `SidebarInset` for a whole app, see the [app sidebar](/vue/components/sidebar#app).

### PageContent

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "fill", type: "boolean", default: "false", description: "The content fills the page and scrolls inside itself, from 768px up" },

The default slot stands in the shared column. All other attributes, listeners included, fall through to the element that scrolls. Its template ref is the component: read the element from `$el`.

### PageFooter

Actions stay below the scrolling `PageContent`. `background` sets how the footer meets the content under it on a phone, as the header's does (default `"soft"`, so the content stays clear under its actions). The footer shares the page column and includes at least 16px of bottom padding, or the device's bottom safe area when larger. Give `Page` a bounded height, such as `100dvh`, to keep the footer at the bottom of the screen. Native div attributes are forwarded to the footer.

### Parts

| Part | Element | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| `PageLeading` | `<div>` | Back or menu controls on the leading side of the bar |
| `PageBar` | `<div>` | Row of the title and the actions |
| `PageHeading` | `<div>` | Title with what stands beside it |
| `PageTitle` | `<h1>` | Page heading |
| `PageSubtitle` | `<div>` | Status of the page, including badges and text |
| `PageActions` | `<div>` | Buttons on the trailing side of the bar |

## Styling

`surface="plain"` is the default mobile canvas. Use `surface="grouped"` for screens of related field groups: light mode uses a gray canvas with white groups; dark mode uses the corresponding base surface levels. This palette is separate from the bars' `background` and leaves desktop colors unchanged.

`PageHeader` and `PageFooter` take `background`: `glass` (the header's default), `soft` (the footer's default), `hard` or `blur`, see [Overview](#overview). Use a hard bar when the content should not show through it on phones.

Page reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--safe-area-inset-top`: shared top inset for Page and Dialog (defaults to `env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px)`); override it for an embedded screen with its own status bar
- `--page-background`: canvas color; mobile follows `surface`, desktop defaults to `var(--color-surface)`
- `--page-bar-gap`: gap between title and actions (default `24px`)
- `--page-bar-height`: bar height (default `36px`)
- `--page-bar-height-phone`: bar height on a phone (default `44px`)
- `--page-bar-hard-line`: hairline of a hard bar (default `var(--page-bar-line)`, as iOS 27.2 draws it)
- `--page-bar-blur-light`, `--page-bar-blur-heavy`: blurs of a blur bar, over the bar and past it, and toward the screen's edge (default `3px`, `12px`); `--page-bar-blur-top-mask`, `--page-bar-blur-top-heavy-mask` and their `-bottom-` pair place them
- `--page-bar-line`: hairline of a glass bar (default `light-dark(rgb(0 0 0 / 10%), var(--color-border))`)
- `--page-bar-veil`: translucent fill of a glass bar, derived from the current mobile canvas
- `--page-content-padding-bottom`: bottom of the content (default `48px`)
- `--page-content-padding-top`: top of the content on a phone (default `16px`)
- `--page-gutter`: side gutter on a phone (default `16px`)
- `--page-gutter-wide`: side gutter and top of the content from 768px up (default `24px`)
- `--page-header-padding-bottom`: gap between the bar and the line (default `12px`)
- `--page-header-padding-top`: top of the header (default `12px`)
- `--page-title-font-size`: title font size (default `20px`)
- `--page-title-line-height`: title line height (default `26px`)
- `--page-width`: width of the column, any value
- `--page-width-column`: `column` width (default `768px`)
- `--page-width-wide`: `wide` width (default `1280px`)

### Footer spacing

`Page.Footer` stays below the scrolling content. Set `--page-footer-gutter` when its primary action needs a larger horizontal inset than the fields. `--page-footer-padding-bottom` controls the space below it, including the safe area.

```css
.mobile-form {
  --page-footer-gutter: 24px;
  --page-footer-padding-bottom: max(24px, env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px));
}
```
