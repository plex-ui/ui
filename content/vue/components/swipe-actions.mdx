Swipe actions keep the actions of a row out of sight until they are wanted, as the swipe actions of an iOS list do: a swipe toward the leading edge reveals the trailing actions, a swipe toward the trailing edge the leading ones, and a full swipe runs the first action of that side. Give a row the actions people take on it often, such as Delete or Archive, and keep the same actions in reach elsewhere too, as in a menu, for people who never swipe.

## API

### SwipeActions

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "leading", type: "SwipeAction[]", default: "[]", description: "Actions revealed by a swipe toward the trailing edge, the first one at the leading edge" },
  { prop: "trailing", type: "SwipeAction[]", default: "[]", description: "Actions revealed by a swipe toward the leading edge, the first one at the trailing edge" },
  { prop: "label-placement", type: '"hidden" | "inside" | "below"', default: '"hidden"', description: "Where the actions show their labels: symbols only, inside the capsule, or below it (for rows of 68px and taller)" },
  { prop: "full-swipe", type: "boolean", default: "true", description: "A swipe past the middle between the open actions and the far edge runs the first action of that side" },
]} />

**Slots**: `default`, the content of the row.

### SwipeAction

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "id", type: "string", description: "Unique ID of the action within its side" },
  { prop: "label", type: "string", description: "Name of the action: shown inside or below the capsule (and on an action without an icon), and its accessible name always" },
  { prop: "Icon", type: "Component", description: "Icon of the action, a component (mark it raw in reactive state)" },
  { prop: "color", type: '"primary" | "secondary" | "danger" | "success" | "info" | "discovery" | "caution" | "warning"', default: '"secondary"', description: "Solid color of the action" },
  { prop: "onSelect", type: "() => void", description: "Called when the action is pressed, or by a full swipe when it is the first action of its side" },
]} />

A swipe starts once the finger has gone 10px across the row and farther across than down; a drag down scrolls the list, and a drag in a text field or along a slider stays with that control. A click that ends a swipe, or that closes an open row, does not reach the content. Each action is also a button, in the order of the screen: the leading actions before the content, the trailing ones after it. The focus on an action reveals its side, and the row closes when the focus leaves it; an action chosen from the keys keeps the focus, so its side stays open. The row has `data-revealed` with the side it shows, and `data-swiping` while a finger moves it.

## Styling

SwipeActions reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--swipe-actions-gap`, `--swipe-actions-edge-inset`, `--swipe-actions-content-gap`: the space between the actions, from the edge, and from the content (default `10px`, `16px`, `10px`)
- `--swipe-actions-action-height`, `--swipe-actions-action-min-width`, `--swipe-actions-action-padding-inline`, `--swipe-actions-icon-size`: a capsule with its symbol only (default `44px`, `60px`, `8px`, `20px`)
- `--swipe-actions-labeled-min-width`, `--swipe-actions-labeled-padding-inline`, `--swipe-actions-labeled-icon-size`, `--swipe-actions-label-gap`: a capsule with its label inside (default `84px`, `4px`, `18px`, `8px`)
- `--swipe-actions-label-font-size`, `--swipe-actions-label-line-height`, `--swipe-actions-label-font-weight`: the labels (default `13px`, `15px`, normal)
- `--swipe-actions-tall-action-height`, `--swipe-actions-tall-padding-top`, `--swipe-actions-tall-padding-bottom`, `--swipe-actions-caption-gap`, `--swipe-actions-caption-font-weight`, `--swipe-actions-caption-color`: a capsule with its label below (default `38px`, `6px`, `4px`, `5px`, medium, the secondary text color)
- `--swipe-actions-background`, `--swipe-actions-content-background`: behind a revealed row and its content, unset by default (on a phone the canvas of the Page or Dialog and the fill of its FieldGroups, on a desktop the secondary surface and the surface)
- `--swipe-actions-content-radius`: the round side of the content (default `9999px`)
- `--swipe-actions-duration`: how long the row takes to open or close (default `250ms`)
