### Overview

<ComponentPreview language="vue" details={<><code>current</code> is the active step, counted from 1: the steps before it are completed and show a check. <code>variant="minimal"</code> draws a compact bar of segments instead, one per step, and takes the count as <code>total</code>; it has no orientation, connector, icons or text, so those settings are for the default variant, and it draws <code>sm</code> as <code>md</code>, so it offers <code>md</code> and <code>lg</code>. <code>orientation="vertical"</code> stacks the steps, for a sidebar or a multi-step form. <code>size</code> sets the scale of the indicators, and <code>color</code> the color scheme of completed and active steps. <code>connector-style</code> draws the lines ahead of the current step solid or dashed; a completed line is solid, so at the last step the setting is not offered. The <code>icon</code> slot (or the <code>icon</code> prop) of a <code>ProgressStepsStep</code> shows a custom icon instead of its number. A step shows a <code>ProgressStepsTitle</code> and a <code>ProgressStepsDescription</code>; leave out the description, or show the indicators alone when the step's details are shown elsewhere. Keep the title then for screen readers with <code>visually-hidden</code>: an icon or a check has no name, and the step takes no room under its indicator.<br /><br />On a phone the four columns of a horizontal row cannot hold a step's description, so the steps stand vertically there and the phone has no <code>orientation</code> setting: <code>useBreakpoint("md")</code> tells which to use. Progress steps have no iOS control of their own; With Navigation shows a compact composition for a phone screen.</>} code={overviewCode} previewMinHeight={168}>

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "icon", type: "Component | VNode | string", description: "Custom icon instead of step number; the icon slot takes precedence" },

**ProgressStepsTitle**: Wrapper for step title text. Applies appropriate styling based on step state.

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "visually-hidden", type: "boolean", default: "false", description: "Hides the title from view and keeps it for screen readers, for steps that show their indicators alone; the step then takes no room under its indicator" },

**ProgressStepsDescription**: Wrapper for step description text. Applies appropriate styling based on step state.

## Styling

Progress Steps reads these component tokens. Override them at any scope to restyle it:

- `--progress-steps-connector-color`: connector color (default `var(--gray-100)`)
- `--progress-steps-connector-color-completed`: connector color completed (default `var(--gray-900)`)
- `--progress-steps-connector-dash-gap`: connector dash gap (default `4px`)
- `--progress-steps-connector-dash-length`: connector dash length (default `4px`)
- `--progress-steps-connector-width`: connector width (default `2px`)
