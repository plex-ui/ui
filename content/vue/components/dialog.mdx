At 767px and below a dialog is one of the two modal views of iOS. `mobile-variant="alert"` (or `mobile-placement="center"`) presents a compact centered glass alert, a confirmation or a request for one short value, with its actions side by side, or stacked from three, and no close icon. Any other dialog is a Sheet, the default, for forms and details: it opens at the bottom with a leading Close button and a centered title, and without a grabber, as iOS 27.2 draws one only on a sheet that changes height. Both variants preserve the desktop appearance.

Two alert actions stand side by side and wrap onto separate rows when their labels need more space; three or more stack, as on iOS. Set `<DialogFooter mobile-layout="stacked">` when two actions should stack too. A title without a message stands centered. Put long sheet content in `DialogBody` to keep the header and actions visible while the body scrolls.

Over a phone keyboard the dialog covers the part of the window the keyboard leaves visible, so a sheet sits on the keyboard and a centered panel stays in view. Inside a [PhoneLayout](/vue/components/phone-layout) it portals into the PhoneLayout box and covers it instead of the window.

**DialogTrigger**: opens the dialog; renders its child element (Reka's `as-child`).

**DialogHeader**: wrapper for title, description and an optional header action. In a phone Sheet the header is the Sheet's Toolbar: Close at the leading edge, the title alone, centered on the 44px row of controls, and the header action at the trailing edge, as in the iOS 27 kit. The description stands under the bar as the first text of the content, at the top of the body when there is one, in the label color of the sheet's text. If the Dialog has its own top Toolbar, that one replaces the header's bar. In a phone Sheet, `background` sets how the bar meets the body scrolled under it: `glass` (the default), `soft`, `hard` or `solid`, as on a [Toolbar](/vue/components/toolbar#background).

**DialogHeaderAction**: positions one toolbar control beside Close on desktop and opposite Close in a mobile sheet. Place it directly inside DialogHeader (a fragment there is fine, a component of your own that returns it is not: a phone Sheet looks for it among the header's own parts to stand it at its edge) and label icon-only controls. Mobile alerts hide this slot.

<PropsTable data={[
  { prop: "side", type: '"leading" | "trailing"', default: '"trailing"', description: "leading puts the action before the title, such as the Back of a step: in a phone Sheet it takes the place of Close, as the back button of a screen pushed inside an iOS sheet" },
]} />

**DialogTitle**: accessible heading for the dialog.

**DialogDescription**: supporting text under the title.

**DialogBody**: the scrolling part between the header and the footer. With it, only the body scrolls. Accepts `class`.

**DialogDescriptionSlot**: where a phone Sheet's description goes in content of your own that takes the place of `DialogBody`, such as a scroller inside a `form` so that Enter submits. Put it first in that content: the description starts it, as it starts `DialogBody`, and it comes before the body's place. Without either, the description stands under the bar in the flow of the Sheet, so keep the bar in flow there. It renders nothing outside a phone Sheet.

**DialogFooter**: footer area for action buttons. Mobile alert actions are 48px pills: two in a wrapping row, three or more stacked. A mobile sheet stacks its actions as an iOS setup sheet: 53px pills, 16px side gutters and at least 16px below them, including the bottom safe area. Its last action, the main one, stands on top, and the order in the page turns with it, so focus and a screen reader meet the actions as they are seen; a soft, outline or ghost action beside a solid one is a plain tinted button under it (red for danger).

## Styling

Use `surface="grouped"` on dialog content for grouped forms. The default `surface="plain"` suits confirmations and standalone content. Mobile dialogs use elevated dark surface levels; the choice is independent of alert/sheet geometry and the bars' `background`.

Dialog reads the shared semantic color, typography and spacing tokens listed in [Design tokens](/docs/foundations/design-tokens), so it follows theme changes made there. Override these Dialog tokens at any scope:

- `--dialog-panel-background`: panel color, and the color of a Sheet's soft, hard and solid bars; mobile follows the selected elevated surface role
- `--dialog-close-background`: close button background (default `transparent`)
- `--dialog-close-background-hover`: close button background on hover
- `--dialog-z-index`: stack order of the overlay and the dialog (default `50`; `z-index` sets it)
- `--dialog-viewport-height`: the height the panel fits in (default `100dvh`; the dialog sets it over a phone keyboard and inside a PhoneLayout)
- `--dialog-steps-background`: background of a step, which covers the one under it as it comes in (default the panel color)

On a phone these tokens set what the Alert and Sheet tokens below leave to them:

- `--dialog-mobile-close-size`: visible close-button circle (default `--control-size-md`, 32px)
- `--dialog-mobile-close-icon-size`: close SVG box (default `--control-icon-size-2xl`, 24px; the glyph occupies about 12px)
- `--dialog-mobile-close-background`: mobile close button fill (default secondary soft)
- `--dialog-mobile-radius`: mobile panel corner radius (default `--radius-4xl`, 24px)
- `--dialog-mobile-action-size`: mobile footer height and close-button touch target (default `--control-size-2xl`, 44px)
- `--dialog-mobile-action-radius`: mobile footer button radius (default `--radius-full`)
- `--dialog-mobile-action-font-size`: mobile footer button text size (default `--control-font-size-lg`)

Alert and Sheet use these additional tokens:

- `--dialog-alert-width`: compact alert width (default `320px`, as a native iOS 27.2 alert; capped by the viewport)
- `--dialog-alert-radius`: alert corner radius (default `34px`)
- `--dialog-alert-padding`: alert outer padding, the inset of the actions; a field stands 1px further out (default `16px`)
- `--dialog-alert-action-height`: minimum Alert action height (default `48px`)
- `--dialog-alert-field-height`: height of a field in the alert on a phone (default `48px`)
- `--dialog-alert-background`, `--dialog-alert-backdrop-filter`, `--dialog-alert-shadow`: alert material on a phone (with `touch.css` white at 65% in light and `--alpha-12` in dark, over `blur(10px)`, with the raised glass shadow)
- `--dialog-alert-header-gap`, `--dialog-alert-header-padding`: space between the title and the message, and around them, so the text stands 31px from the alert's sides (default `5.67px`, `5px 15px 5.5px`)
- `--dialog-alert-message-color`, `--dialog-alert-message-size`, `--dialog-alert-message-leading`: alert message on a phone (default `var(--color-text)`, `15px`, `20px`; with `touch.css` the secondary label)
- `--dialog-alert-fill`: fill of a soft Cancel and a soft field in the alert (with `touch.css` `--alpha-15` in light and `--alpha-12` in dark)
- `--dialog-alert-action-gap`, `--dialog-alert-action-font-weight`, `--dialog-alert-preferred-font-weight`: gap between the actions, the weight of an action and of a solid one other than danger (default `8px`, `510`, `590`)
- `--dialog-sheet-action-height`: minimum Sheet action height (default `50px`)
- `--dialog-sheet-radius`: sheet top corner radius (default `38px`; fullscreen stays square)
- `--dialog-mobile-scrim`: dim behind a phone Sheet or Alert (default black at 20% in light and 50% in dark, as native iOS 26); desktop keeps `rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`
- `--dialog-sheet-close-size`: sheet close-button circle and touch target (default `--control-size-2xl`, 44px)
- `--dialog-sheet-close-icon-size`: sheet close SVG box (default `34px`; the visible cross is about 17px)
- `--dialog-mobile-type-size`: Alert and Sheet heading and actions, and the Sheet description; the Alert message takes `--dialog-alert-message-size` (default `17px`)
- `--dialog-mobile-type-leading`: line height of the Alert and Sheet heading and actions, and of the Sheet description (default `22px`)
