<ComponentPreview className={phoneRowsStyles.PhoneRowsPreview} language="vue" details={<>SegmentedControl provides two visual variants: <code>segmented</code> (default) with a sliding background highlight, and <code>underline</code> with an animated line indicator. Both support horizontal and vertical orientations. Bind the selected tab with <code>v-model:value</code>; the examples keep it in a <code>tab</code> variable. <code>disabled</code> affects the whole group, which on a phone shows at half opacity when horizontal, as a disabled iOS segmented control does; <code>monthDisabled</code> disables only Month, whose label on a phone takes the disabled label of iOS, 20% of the label color. Arrow-key navigation skips disabled tabs without resetting the selected value. On a phone horizontal tabs of either variant are the iOS 27 segmented control, filling the row of a phone screen, in its two sizes. The default <code>md</code> and larger are the large control of iOS 27 apps, as Calendar and Reminders draw it and SwiftUI draws a segmented Picker in a toolbar: a 48px glass capsule, a 40px gray selection 4px inside it, 8px between segments, every label 15px Medium and primary. <code>sm</code> and smaller are the small one, the plain control of SwiftUI's Picker in content and of Safari's bookmarks: a 32px capsule of the tertiary fill, a white selection 2px inside it, labels 13px, Semibold when selected. <code>mobileMaterial</code> switches the look, as for Button: <code>flat</code> makes the large one plain, 49px as SwiftUI's <code>.controlSize(.large)</code>, and <code>glass</code> puts the small one under the Liquid Glass of <code>.glassEffect()</code>. Inside a phone Toolbar every control is the large glass one. ### Also exported as Tabs

The same component is exported as `Tabs`, with `TabsTab` for an option, for code that names it so:

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import { ref } from "vue"
import { Tabs, TabsTab } from "@plexui/vue/components/Tabs"

const mode = ref("a")
</script>

<template>
  <Tabs v-model:value="mode" aria-label="Mode">
    <TabsTab value="a">A</TabsTab>
  </Tabs>
</template>
```

`SegmentedControlOption`, `SegmentedControlTab`, `TabsOption` and `TabsTab` are one component.
